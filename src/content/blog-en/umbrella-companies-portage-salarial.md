That's it, you've finally decided to go freelance, but now comes the question of legal status… *Micro-entreprise*, *Entreprise Individuelle*, *Entreprise Individuelle à Responsabilité Limitée*, *Entreprise Unipersonnelle à Responsabilité Limitée*, *Société Par Actions Simplifiée Unipersonnelle* (France's various sole-trader and single-owner company forms)… So why not *portage salarial* (France's umbrella company arrangement)?  

‍

This last option is often little known, and yet it comes with many advantages.

Never heard of it? That's normal, it's reserved for the best ;) This status, written into the French Labour Code by the ordinance of 2 April 2015, is intended for service providers who demonstrate expertise in a field of activity and a high degree of autonomy.

Halfway between independent and salaried worker, this status lets you combine all the advantages: the freedom to work however you like, on your own account, while keeping the security of a salaried worker, including unemployment insurance if things go wrong.

‍  

By choosing this status, there's no need to wait before you start working: the administrative formalities can be completed in just 24 hours.

Want more? No more end-of-month stress, chasing invoices or scrambling for cash flow! The *portage* company takes care of everything. You'll receive a payslip at the end of each month, essential when you have a property project ;)

‍  

More than 90,000 independents have chosen to work under *portage*. Their combined revenue represents nearly €1.5 billion. The health crisis boosted the number of career changes and the desire to find a new work/life balance. Add to that the spread of remote working: you no longer need to live in the same city as your clients.

All of these factors favour freelance activity, and *portage salarial* is no exception.

‍  

In short, *portage salarial* means:

-   starting work within 24 hours
-   zero admin (no company registration, no accounting, nothing!)
-   being covered by professional liability insurance
-   no revenue ceiling
-   deducting business expenses and recovering VAT
-   enjoying the same social security cover as salaried workers (unemployment insurance, sickness, health, pension, etc.)
-   building and managing your own client base  
    

What if I've already set up my own structure? No problem! Legal statuses can be combined. If you've reached the €72,600 revenue ceiling for a *micro-entreprise* and want to bill some of your clients through a *portage salarial* company, that's possible. Or if you have *Pôle emploi* (France's public job-seeker agency) benefits you want to use up, still possible! The salary generated by your engagements can be paid to you on request, in one or several instalments: you choose the amount on your payslip.
