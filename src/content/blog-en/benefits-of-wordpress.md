[**Designing or redesigning a website**](/en/projects/website-redesign) has become a real challenge for companies of all sizes. Among the many platforms available, WordPress stands out as one of the market-leading tools.

Easy to use, WordPress offers great flexibility for customisation, from themes to plugins, so you can achieve a unique, professional design. Let's see whether this CMS could suit you.

## **What is WordPress and why is it so popular?**

WordPress is a content management system (CMS) that makes it easy to create and manage websites. Launched in 2003, it quickly grew in popularity. Originally designed as a blogging platform, WordPress has evolved over the years into a powerful tool for building websites of all kinds, from blogs to e-commerce sites to corporate portals. This evolution has helped make WordPress one of the most widely used CMSs in the world, with more than 40% of websites on the internet running on the platform (that's no small thing).

One of the main reasons for WordPress's popularity lies in its community. Thousands of developers and designers regularly contribute to improving the software by creating new themes, plugins and security updates. The community also offers a wide range of resources, forums and tutorials, making learning and troubleshooting much simpler for users, whether they're beginners or experienced.

## **Why choose WordPress to build your site?**

One of WordPress's main advantages is its ease of use. Even without any particular technical skills, you can create a professional website fairly quickly. The interface is intuitive and lets users easily manage their content, add images and change the layout without touching the code. This allows entrepreneurs and small businesses to focus on their activity without worrying about the technical side of building a website.

Another advantage is WordPress's great flexibility. Thanks to a multitude of themes and plugins, users can tailor their website to their specific needs. Whether you want to add contact forms, booking systems or e-commerce features, there's a plugin for it. This customisation makes it possible to create a pleasant, distinctive user experience.

WordPress is also optimised for [**search engine optimisation (SEO)**](/en/roles/freelance-seo-consultant) – in fact, it's often the first advantage WordPress users point to. The built-in features and available plugins make it easy to optimise titles, meta descriptions and image tags, which improves the site's visibility on search engines. WordPress generates clean, structured code, which is good for SEO and so brings more visitors to your site.

## **WordPress themes**

WordPress themes determine the overall look of the site, including the layout, colours, fonts and styles. There are thousands of themes available, each offering varied designs ranging from minimalist to more complex. Choosing the right theme can make a significant difference to how visitors perceive your company. There are free ones, which are fairly basic, and tons of paid ones, which are more complex and modern.

Another aspect to consider is theme customisation. Most premium themes are highly customisable, letting you change elements without any web development skills, but that's not true of every theme, and some people can sometimes find themselves a bit stuck. In that case, the best thing is to bring in [**a web developer**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer) if you feel you need more customisation.

WordPress themes are often already optimised for SEO. They are structured to make indexing by search engines easier. By choosing a well-designed theme, you lay the foundations for a website that not only attracts visitors but also performs well in terms of SEO, which is very important for your company's long-term success.

## **WordPress plugins to optimise your website's functionality and appearance**

WordPress plugins are extensions that add extra features and options to your site. With tens of thousands of plugins available, you can easily add tools to improve performance, design and the overall user experience. For example, plugins such as WooCommerce turn a WordPress site into a complete online shop, with product management, payment and shipping features.

Some plugins focus on optimising the site's appearance. Page builders such as Elementor or Beaver Builder let you create custom layouts using a simple drag-and-drop system. This allows users to design unique pages without programming skills, making content creation more visually appealing and interactive.

Plugins can also improve your site's security and performance. Tools such as Wordfence or Sucuri offer security features that protect your site against potential threats. Caching plugins such as W3 Total Cache help improve page loading speed, for example.

WordPress offers several plugins that make SEO optimisation easier. Among the most popular, Yoast SEO and All in One SEO Pack let you optimise title tags and meta descriptions and even analyse the readability of your content. These tools guide you in creating content that meets search engines' criteria, increasing your chances of appearing as high as possible in search results.

## **Resources and tutorials to learn how to use WordPress**

If you want to get to grips with WordPress, there's a wealth of resources and tutorials available. The official WordPress site offers documentation covering everything from the basics to advanced features. This resource is a great starting point for beginners looking to understand the platform's fundamentals.

Alongside that, many specialist blogs and YouTube channels offer guides and video tutorials on using WordPress. These resources can be particularly useful for people who learn best by doing, and almost all of them are free. Platforms such as Udemy and Coursera also offer full WordPress courses.

And if needed, forums such as Reddit, Stack Overflow or Facebook groups dedicated to WordPress are perfect for asking questions and swapping tips with other users.
