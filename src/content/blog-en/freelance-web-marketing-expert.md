# Web marketing strategies and development for maximum visibility with a freelance expert

Have you ever thought about hiring a freelance web marketing expert to optimise your online presence? Nowadays, digital marketing and online sales techniques are essential for increasing a company's visibility and reputation on the internet. However, it can be difficult to find the right qualified profiles, able to put effective, lasting strategies in place while respecting your initial objectives. A freelance web marketing consultant is therefore a key solution, able to help companies turn their advertising around and grow digitally thanks to their multidisciplinary skills (SEO/SEA, ad campaigns, relevant content, etc.).

## What is a freelance web marketing expert?

A freelance web marketing expert is a consultant specialising in digital marketing and online sales techniques, who helps companies improve their visibility on the internet. They provide advice on the project's overall strategy and can also offer services such as [organic search](/en/roles/freelance-seo-consultant) and paid search (SEO/SEA), online advertising and content development.

The skills needed to be a web marketing expert are varied: good analytical skills, an advanced command of computer languages, in-depth knowledge of digital tools and techniques, experience of working online and the ability to communicate with a range of contacts. Mastering these skills is essential to guarantee the project's success.

Working with a freelance web marketing consultant has several advantages: put your company in a favourable position on search engines; gain better visibility on the main social networks; benefit from professional, specialist expertise; optimise your costs thanks to freelance rates; enjoy the flexibility and speed of execution this type of collaboration allows.

However, there are certain limits to the relationship with a freelance web marketing consultant. First of all, the absence of a legal framework when you engage an independent provider who works exclusively for your company.

In addition, there can sometimes be a lack of transparency or accountability when it comes to monitoring projects or achieving the objectives set.

## How to find a freelance web marketing expert

There are several ways to find a web marketing expert: searching the web, looking on specialist social networks, using freelance marketplaces, or contacting a specialist talent agency.

### Online search

The first step is to carry out general searches online to find the ideal consultant. The main search engines will be useful here: Google, Yahoo! or Bing can provide relevant results.

You can also use specialist platforms such as LinkedIn to find available, qualified freelancers.

### Social media

Social networks are an excellent resource for finding web marketing specialists. Twitter, Facebook and Instagram are the most popular, but there are other platforms, such as Pinterest or YouTube, that can be used effectively.

For example, you can search by hashtags, reach out to specialist communities or contact the consultant directly.

### Freelance marketplaces

There are sites specialising in connecting companies with freelancers, such as Freelancer, Upwork or Fiverr, which offer a wide range of highly qualified services. These platforms can help you find the perfect profile for your project and give you access to a variety of profiles.

### Talent agencies

If you're looking for a web marketing expert with specific experience and thorough training, talent agencies are the best option. Agencies have particular know-how and can provide consultants able to manage complex, technically ambitious projects.

## How to work with a freelance web marketing expert

Once you've found the ideal consultant for your project, it's time to talk it through and draw up a clear, consistent contract between the two parties. This will help the freelancer understand exactly what you need in terms of developments, strategies and expected results.

### Drawing up a contract

The first thing to consider when working with a web marketing consultant is drawing up a solid contract that covers all the necessary considerations for the project: rates, payment terms, rights to use the applications the provider will have access to, the freelancer's responsibilities and the limits of their commitments, etc. A good contract ensures legal certainty for both parties involved in the collaboration.
