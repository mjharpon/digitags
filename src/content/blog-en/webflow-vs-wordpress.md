## **What are the main differences between Webflow and WordPress?**

![wordpress freelance webflow dgitags ](/uploads/blog/webflow-vs-wordpress-quelle-est-la-meilleure-plateforme-de-creation-de-site-web-en-2021-0.webp)

1/ WordPress is limited by **templates** or is **coded from scratch**, whereas Webflow gives you **total design freedom**.

2/ Webflow has **clean, good-quality code**, whereas WordPress code can quickly become **cluttered** because of the heavy need for plugins.

3/ Webflow lets you make **changes on the page**, whereas with WordPress you have to use a **dashboard** and **page editors**.

4/ WordPress is **free**. You only have to pay for your hosting. Unlike Webflow, which is **paid** and more expensive depending on the subscription you choose.

5/ Webflow has a **drag-and-drop** page builder, whereas WordPress requires a **plugin** to enable one.

Webflow and WordPress are therefore two major competitors. However, some differences between them could help you make your choice.  

### ‍**Introducing Webflow**

![wordpress freelance webflow dgitags design](/uploads/blog/webflow-vs-wordpress-quelle-est-la-meilleure-plateforme-de-creation-de-site-web-en-2021-1.webp)

Webflow is a **cloud-based website builder** that lets anyone create a website without needing programming skills or knowledge. Webflow is much more **powerful** and **customisable** than other website-building platforms such as Squarespace and Wix.

### **What are Webflow's features?**

Webflow lets you **take full control of HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript**, so you can use the visual page builder to create exactly what you need. Once you've built the perfect website, you can publish it directly to the web or download the code and hand it over to your developers.

The page builder uses a **drag-and-drop** **interface**, so you can start with a blank canvas and simply drag and drop all the elements, such as background videos, sliders, tabs and images, wherever you want. You can **view the page structure** of your website in the browser and restructure the elements you need in a few clicks.

All pages and elements come with **responsive design**. You can adjust and preview layouts for any device. People visiting your site will therefore enjoy the **best user experience**, whatever type of device they use.

Unlike other website builders, you have **full control** over your typography. You can **use the font of your choice and customise everything**, from line height to tracking.

Webflow also lets you **define global colour swatches**, so you can change every instance of a colour across your website in a single click, guaranteeing perfect consistency across all your pages.

### **Webflow: an interactive tool?**

Webflow lets you **create fully customised interactions and animations** across your entire site without adding a line of code. The possibilities are therefore limitless.

For example, you can create **scroll**-based animations, meaning any element can be linked to scroll progress, so that all the elements you want to include can be revealed according to how far the user has scrolled.

You can also create animations that build up as the user scrolls, so that when the user reaches the right place on the page, the image can be seen in full.

You can also include **content revealed on click** or **hover animations** that reveal new content when you move your mouse over a certain area.

If these animations seem too complex, there are also **20 simpler preset animations** you can add to your site in a few clicks.

### **Webflow: a content management tool**

Webflow has a **very comprehensive CMS** that suits professional designers just as well as developers or content managers. It's easy to create a well-structured content model and connect it to your site's design.

You can **design content directly on the page**, so you can see what it will look like as you build it, or display content conditionally by adding filters. That way, depending on certain fields, only the necessary content is displayed.

You can also build the **search function from scratch**, so that it displays content exactly the way you want it to be seen.

Finally, you can give your clients and team members **access to the site** so they can get in and make the changes they want. You can even collaborate with team members in real time.

### **E-commerce with Webflow**

![wordpress freelance webflow dgitags design](/uploads/blog/webflow-vs-wordpress-quelle-est-la-meilleure-plateforme-de-creation-de-site-web-en-2021-2.webp)

As we've already said, Webflow makes it easy to **design your website**, **sell your products** and **grow your business** by giving you full control over everything. Every page can be fully **customised**. For example, your checkout page can match the rest of your website.

Customers can pay for their products with **several payment methods**, including Stripe, Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal. You can choose where and how you ship your products by setting shipping rules and regions.

The order management dashboard is easy to use. Once you're ready to ship your orders, you can quickly and easily **print your shipping labels** thanks to the **Zapier** integration.

You can also **customise the transactional emails** sent to your customers to confirm their purchase, to make sure everything is 100% on-brand. You can also add custom animations and interactions to stand out from your competitors.

### **The advantages of using Webflow**

o  Freedom to create fully customised websites without needing to code.

o  A drag-and-drop page builder lets you build and customise your pages, all in the same page interface.

o  Easy creation of animations and interactions without needing to code

o  Websites are easy to optimise for search engines.

o  Make changes to your page directly on the page, so you can see exactly what it will look like before publishing it.

o  Invite collaborators to edit and review your site's content.

o  Powerful hosting with minimal downtime for your visitors

o  SSL certificate included

o  A growing library of video tutorials

### **The drawbacks of using Webflow**

o  A short learning curve, especially if you've never done web development before

o  More expensive than WordPress

o  Features still in development (POS integrations, discounts, coupons, subscription sales, digital products)

## **Introducing WordPress in 2021**

![wordpress freelance webflow dgitags design](/uploads/blog/webflow-vs-wordpress-quelle-est-la-meilleure-plateforme-de-creation-de-site-web-en-2021-7.webp)

 WordPress is **open-source software**, completely **free** for everyone; you just need to **pay for your hosting**. You can use WordPress to create blogs, websites, an online shop or to showcase your portfolio projects.

### **Designing your website with WordPress**

It's relatively easy to create your website with WordPress. You can use one of the thousands of **free or paid themes available**. WordPress has its own theme directory, but you can also buy themes on other websites such as **Themeforest** or **Creativemarket**.

Once your theme is installed, you can customise some of its elements such as colours, fonts, sizes and images. You can also **use plugins** to add extra features that aren't already included in your theme.

Unfortunately, **if you want a fully customised website, you'll need to work with a [web developer](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer)**. They have full access to the WordPress theme developer handbook, which will give you everything you need to know about WordPress theme development.   

### **WordPress: a content management tool**

WordPress **makes it easy to create and publish** your content online. You don't need to know how to code to use WordPress's basic features (e.g. publishing). You can manage everything from your WordPress dashboard, which is **intuitive** and **easy to use**. The menu bar on the left lets you navigate between the different pages and areas of your website. WordPress really is a tool **accessible to everyone**.

WordPress gives you the option to **add as many pages to your site as you like**, so you can create a simple one- or two-page site or a more complex site with hundreds of pages.

If you need to add other users and authors to your site, that's not a problem. WordPress has **multi-user capabilities**, and you can add new users by choosing from six different roles with different capabilities and access permissions, including author, administrator, editor, contributor and subscriber.

WordPress is actually an ideal platform for small and large companies alike, as it can easily evolve as your business grows.

So it's very quick and easy to get started with WordPress. You can install the software in a few minutes, then add a template and content. If you move fast, your site can be up and running in less than an hour.

### **E-commerce with WordPress**

![wordpress freelance webflow dgitags design](/uploads/blog/webflow-vs-wordpress-quelle-est-la-meilleure-plateforme-de-creation-de-site-web-en-2021-8.webp)

 To create an online shop with WordPress, you need to **install a free e-commerce plugin** (e.g. 28% of all online shops run on **WooCommerce**. A customisable plugin.).

### ‍**The advantages of using WordPress**

\- Free to use. You just need to pay for hosting.

\- A fully customisable tool. You have full control over your site and all your data.

\- Thousands of extensions and plugins are available

\- Easy to use, accessible to everyone, with no need to code

\- There are already thousands of integrations available for WordPress, so whatever tools you use to run your business, there's a good chance they work with WordPress.

\- Creation of SEO-optimised websites (many third-party plugins offer optimal SEO settings). WordPress is mobile-friendly.

### **The drawbacks of using WordPress**

\- If you want a fully customised design, you'll have to code it yourself or work with a developer.

\- You're responsible for all updates and maintenance of your website, since your site belongs to you and is managed by you (unless you take advantage of managed WordPress hosting).
