## **The benefits of hiring a freelancer**

Hiring freelancers is an option suited to companies of all sizes. Flexibility is one of the main strengths of this approach: where a traditional hire on a *CDI* (France's permanent contract) takes 6 months on average, it takes 6 days for a freelancer. Freelancers can work hours suited to the specific needs of projects, letting you adapt quickly to fluctuations in demand. By engaging freelancers, you can also adjust your human resources to match current needs, without the constraints of permanent staff.

Access to a wide range of skills is another undeniable advantage of hiring independent workers. Freelance platforms make it possible to find experts in a huge variety of fields. This lets you select specific talent with the skills you need for each project, without having to go through long, costly hiring processes.

Hiring freelancers can also help reduce overall costs for companies. Freelancers are often paid per engagement or by the hour, which means you only pay for the work done. This can prove more economical than maintaining full-time staff, especially for short-term projects. The costs associated with taking on permanent staff, such as social security contributions, don't apply when you hire freelancers. This approach therefore lets you maximise your return on investment while accessing high-quality skills.

## **The best platforms to hire freelancers**

Several platforms stand out for their reputation, ease of use and the quality of the talent they offer. Each has its own unique features, suited to different types of projects and budgets. Unlike the other platforms, [**digitags.work**](/en) is a talent agent. ### **Malt**

[**Malt**](https://www.malt.fr/) positions itself as a marketplace for freelance talent. Its popularity rests on the diversity of skills on offer and the ease of use of its interface. As a company, you can post job listings specifying your needs in terms of skills, budget and deadlines. Interested freelancers can then submit their applications.

Malt includes a rating and review system. Freelancers are rated by clients on the basis of their performance, which can help you make your choice.

### **Freelancer**

[**Freelancer**](https://www.fr.freelancer.com/) is a platform similar to Malt. It gives access to a wide range of freelancers specialising in various fields, from web development to copywriting and digital marketing. It's a global platform, which can be useful if you're looking for talent abroad.
