# **Tips for writing a standout CV that makes a difference with hiring managers**

In today's world of work, mastering digital skills has become essential to stand out to hiring managers. A standout **digital CV** can be a valuable asset in landing your dream job. In this article, we'll guide you through the key steps to create a digital CV that catches hiring managers' attention and showcases your skills.

## **Why a digital CV?**

A digital CV is an online version of your traditional curriculum vitae, adapted to the requirements of the digital sector. It lets you not only present your skills and experience, but also include interactive elements such as links to your work or online projects. What's more, a digital CV offers better visibility on search engines like Google, increasing your chances of being spotted by hiring managers.

## **The key elements of a standout digital CV**

To create a successful digital CV, it's important to take several key elements into account:

1.  **Choice of keywords**: use relevant keywords to optimise your CV and increase its visibility on search engines.
2.  **Layout and design**: opt for a clear, attractive design that makes it easy to read and holds the hiring manager's attention.
3.  **Digital skills**: highlight your technical skills and how you apply them in a professional context.
4.  **Tailoring to the role you're targeting**: adapt your CV to the sector and role you're aiming for, highlighting the most relevant skills and experience.

## **How to choose the right keywords for your digital CV**

To optimise your digital CV, it's essential to select relevant keywords. Here are a few tips to help you:

-   Analyse job postings in your field to identify the most sought-after skills and qualifications.
-   Use keyword research tools such as Google AdWords or Ubersuggest to identify popular terms related to your sector.
-   Work these keywords into your CV, particularly in the headings, the summary and the description of your skills.

## **Layout and design of a digital CV**

Good design helps make your digital CV attractive and easy to read. Here are a few tips for getting the layout right:

-   Choose a clear, legible font, such as Arial or Calibri.
-   Use bullet points to present your skills and experience concisely.
-   Separate the different sections with clearly visible headings and white space.
-   Add links to your online work, such as portfolios, blog posts or personal projects.

## **How to highlight your digital skills**

Showcasing your digital skills is crucial to catching hiring managers' attention in the digital sector. Here's how to go about it:

-   Identify your technical skills (programming languages, software, etc.) and soft skills (communication, team spirit) related to digital.
-   Present these skills in a dedicated section of your CV, illustrating them with concrete examples of how you've applied them in a professional context.

## **Tailoring your digital CV to the role you're targeting**

It's important to adapt your digital CV to the role you're aiming for. Here are a few tips to help you do so:

-   Analyse the job posting and identify the skills and qualifications required.
-   Highlight the experience and achievements that best match the requirements of the role.
-   Adapt your summary to underline your interest in, and strengths for, the role you're targeting.
-   Add a portfolio of your work to give hiring managers concrete examples.

By following these tips, you'll be able to create a standout digital CV that showcases your skills and helps you stand out to hiring managers. Don't forget that a well-designed CV tailored to the requirements of the digital sector considerably increases your chances of landing your dream job. So don't hesitate: put these tips into practice and make your digital profile shine!
