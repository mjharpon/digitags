Looking for one or more independent professionals for your next project? Working with a freelance collective might be the perfect solution for you – let's check that together in this article.

When you work with a freelance collective, you get a complete team of specialists across different fields. They operate a bit like an agency, give or take a few details.

They can adapt to your needs and step in at different stages of your project. Let's look at the advantages this could bring depending on your needs!

## What is a freelance collective?

A freelance collective is a group of several freelancers (you'd probably guessed that much) from different areas of expertise, who work together to pool their skills, share mission opportunities and take on somewhat bigger projects. Unlike agencies, these collectives operate without a hierarchy, allowing each member to keep their independence while enjoying the benefits of teamwork. A freelance collective can be a great alternative to the traditional dev agency. Here are its advantages.

## **The benefits of freelance collectives**

### **Flexibility: adapting to your project's needs**

Flexibility is one of the main strengths of working with a freelance collective. Freelancers are often able to adapt quickly to the specific requirements of each project. Whatever phase you're in – design, development or even implementation – the collective can adjust the workload to your priorities. This can be particularly useful if your project evolves or if something unexpected comes up.

Freelancers are generally good at handling several projects at once. They know how to juggle various clients and deadlines, so they're talent with a kind of agility you don't always find in an agency's permanent staff, for example.

### **Expertise: benefiting from varied skills**

Working with a freelance collective gives you access to several talents with different skills and expertise. Each freelancer has their own area of specialisation ([**web development**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer), [**design**](/en/roles/freelance-ui-designer), [**copywriting**](/en/roles/freelance-seo-consultant) or digital marketing). This lets you build a tailor-made team, adapted as closely as possible to your project's specific needs.

With a collective, projects benefit from varied perspectives, which can lead to more innovative and effective solutions. For example, a graphic designer working with a copywriter and a marketing expert can develop an advertising campaign that not only grabs attention visually but also communicates a clear, compelling message. This synergy between different areas of expertise is often hard to achieve with an in-house team limited to specific skills.

### **Lower costs**

Working with a freelance collective is often more economical than taking on permanent staff or working with an agency. Salaries, social charges, insurance and other benefits can quickly add up (I'm probably not telling you anything new). By opting for a freelance collective, you only pay for the work delivered, which gives you better control over your budget while still accessing top-level skills. It's therefore particularly recommended for SMEs or startups that need to manage their resources carefully and/or efficiently.

Freelance collectives are often less expensive because of their members' independent status. They're free to set their rates according to their expertise and market demand, which can lead to more competitive prices. When working with a collective, you can also negotiate group rates or packages that let you maximise your ROI.

Rather than committing long term to permanent staff on a project that may only need resources at a given moment, you can adjust your budget to your needs.
