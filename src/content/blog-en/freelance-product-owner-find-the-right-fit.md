## **What is a freelance Product Owner?**

A Product Owner (PO) is a person who manages the design and delivery of a digital product with an IT dimension, such as websites, apps or software. The PO steers the delivery of digital projects using [**agile practices**](/en/roles/freelance-agile-coach) and Scrum to manage product development and the backlog.

A freelance Product Owner is an independent professional with in-depth expertise in project management and in a specific field. As project leader, their role is to understand the client's needs, define a clear vision for the product and work closely with the development team to ensure the objectives set are achieved.

## **Skills of a freelance Product Owner**

A good Product Owner must have solid knowledge of project management and agile methodologies such as Scrum. They must be able to set clear objectives, plan and prioritise tasks, and closely monitor the project's progress.

A freelance Product Owner must have excellent communication skills. They must understand the client's needs and communicate them effectively to the development team. They must give constructive feedback and encourage collaboration within the team.

In addition, a good Product Owner must be results-oriented and have strong analytical skills. They make decisions based on the data available and adjust the project strategy accordingly.

## **Freelance Product Owner rates**

The rates and pay of a freelance Product Owner can vary depending on several factors, such as experience, skills and project duration. In general, freelance Product Owners bill their services by the hour, by the day or by the project.

A freelance Product Owner's hourly rates can range from €50 to more than €150, depending on their expertise and the complexity of the project. Some Product Owners may also offer fixed prices for short projects or day rates for longer projects.
