You have a project and you want to engage a freelancer to help you deliver it. But how do you find the best freelancer to match your needs? At [**Digitags**](/en) we place freelancers with clients every day, so I'm going to share all the tips for finding the perfect match.

Whether you need a talented [**web developer**](/en/tech-and-data), [**designer**](/en/design) or [**marketer**](/en/digital-marketing), I'll give you my advice on where to look, how to assess a freelancer's skills, how to agree on a rate, and much more.

## **Criteria for finding the best independent professional for your project**

At the risk of preaching to the converted, I'll at least reassure you in your decision. Working with a freelancer has many advantages (and I'm not just saying that because I am one). To make the most of them, you obviously need to find the right one. So here are a few criteria to consider in your search.

### **Define the skills and services required**

When looking for the best freelancer for your project, it's important to take certain key criteria into account. First of all, make sure you clearly define your needs and expectations for the project. You'll need to determine which specific qualities are required to carry out the mission.

To do this, look carefully at every aspect of the project and list the technical, analytical and conceptual requirements that will be needed. You should also assess the complexity of the work and decide whether it should be done by one freelancer or several. At Digitags, we also offer the expertise of [**our freelance collectives**](/en/studios-and-collectives).

### **Define the budget and the time needed**

Once you have a clear idea of the services you're looking for, you can set a budget for your project. A freelancer is an expert who can deliver a project faster than a salaried member of staff, and to a high standard. Add up the time a staff member might spend on the mission, potential training, possible mistakes due to lack of experience: all these factors can amount to a higher total cost than hiring a freelancer. We talk about this in our article on [**the advantages of hiring a freelancer rather than a salaried member of staff**](/en/blog/freelancer-vs-permanent-hire).

Next, you need to consider how the project will be divided up and how much time will be needed to complete it. Don't forget that any extension of the timeline will have a direct impact on your budget.

## **Where to find qualified freelancers**

Once you've clearly defined the type of service you're looking for and all the requirements and constraints, you can start looking for a competent, reliable freelancer. There are several places where you can find qualified freelancers for your project.

### **Use matching platforms**

The purpose of freelancing platforms like [**Digitags**](/en) is to make it easier to find and select a freelancer from a huge pool of candidates. These sites give you access to thousands of freelancers all over the world.

You can choose candidates according to their qualifications, skills, rates and location. Most platforms also provide resources to help you find the right freelancer for your specific needs. This type of service is ideal if you're looking for an experienced professional who can take on your project.

### **Use social media to find freelancers**

Professional social networks such as LinkedIn can also be an excellent resource for finding qualified freelancers. You can search for profiles that match your criteria and get in touch directly with people who seem to have the skills your project requires.

LinkedIn profiles often give a more detailed description of the freelancer. You can also use Twitter and Instagram to follow a freelancer's work and get a better sense of their abilities. This kind of research takes time, but it's effective. If you're short on time, we can help!

### Get a freelancer recommended to you

Don't forget to ask your colleagues, friends or members of your professional network for recommendations. They may know trusted freelancers who have already proven themselves on similar projects.

## **How to assess a freelancer's skills and experience**

When assessing a freelancer's skills and experience, it's important to consider several aspects.

Just as when you choose a restaurant, you can (or should) check potential freelancers' references and reviews. Look at the recommendations left by other clients, as well as the work shown in their portfolio, to get an idea of the quality of their work and their reliability. If they don't have a portfolio, don't hesitate to ask for samples of their work to assess their style and level of expertise.

Ask them how they solved specific problems and what results they achieved. An in-depth discussion will give you a better understanding of their working approach and their ability to rise to challenges.

## **Questions to ask when interviewing an independent professional**

When interviewing a freelancer, it's important to ask the right questions to assess whether they're right for your project. Here are some key questions to ask:

1.  What is your experience in this field, and what are your specific areas of expertise?
2.  Have you already worked on a similar mission, and can you tell us about it?
3.  When can you start? And what deadline and timeline could we consider?
4.  What are your preferred communication methods, and how often can you provide updates on the project's progress?
5.  How do you handle problems or obstacles that might arise during the project?
6.  What resources do we need to provide you with?
7.  What are your rates, and what are your payment terms?

## **Negotiating rates and deadlines with a freelancer**

Time is money, and money... is money. When negotiating rates and deadlines with a freelancer, it's important to be clear about your expectations and your budget constraints.

Be open to discussion and ready to find a compromise. The freelancer may also have specific requirements in terms of rates and deadlines, so it's important to take these into account in the negotiation.

Once you've agreed on rates and deadlines, make sure you put them in writing in a formal contract. This will protect both parties and clearly set expectations for the project.

## **Hire the best freelancer for your project**

When you're thinking about [**hiring a freelancer**](/en/hire-a-freelancer), it's essential to take a structured approach to maximise your chances of success. Unlike traditional permanent hires, bringing in a freelancer gives you flexibility and immediate expertise without a long-term commitment. Before starting your search, clearly define the skills you're looking for, your project's goals, and the budget you're prepared to allocate. Specialist platforms and social media are powerful tools for getting in touch with qualified freelancers. However, a rigorous selection process and good communication from the outset are vital to ensure a fruitful collaboration and avoid surprises.

Finding the best freelancer for your project holds no more secrets for you: you can now make informed decisions and find the ideal candidate. Take the time to define your needs, assess potential freelancers' skills and experience, ask the right questions in interviews, and negotiate rates and deadlines fairly.
