1.  **Write a rock-solid brief**

2.  **Choose the right platform**

4.  **Frame the engagement**

5.  **Onboard like a pro**

## Managing the legal and financial risks of **freelance hiring**

-   ***Portage salarial*** (France's umbrella-company scheme): ideal when internal compliance is strict.
-   **Intellectual property clauses**: specify the transfer of rights from the quote stage.
-   ***URSSAF*** **status (France's social contributions body) & VAT**: check in 3 clicks on digitags.work which regime applies.
-   **Professional liability insurance (*RC Pro*)**: essential for IT & marketing data freelancers.

## Working with freelancers FAQ: your frequently asked questions

### 1\. How do you set the budget for a freelance engagement?

Count the **total cost of ownership**: person-days × duration + software + management. A senior Growth Hacker bills an average of €550 excl. VAT per day. Benchmark against public rate grids, then adjust according to how rare the skill is.

### 2\. Should you sign an external contract or go through the freelancer directly?

digitags.work generates a compliant contract + IP transfer clauses. You only pay one consolidated invoice, which simplifies your accounting.

### 3\. How do you assess a marketing freelancer's performance?

Set **OKRs** from the kick-off and follow a shared dashboard (clicks, leads, CAC). Hold a 30-minute monthly _retro_ to adjust the objectives. digitags.work provides a ready-to-use KPI template.
