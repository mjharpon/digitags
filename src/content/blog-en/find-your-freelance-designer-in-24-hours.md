## **Criteria to consider when choosing a freelance designer**

When you choose a [**freelance designer**](/en/blog/freelance-designer-find-the-right-professional) on [**digitags.work**](/en), it's important to consider certain criteria to make sure you make the best possible choice. Here are a few things to bear in mind:

1.  Experience and skills: Check the designer's portfolio to make sure they have relevant experience in the area of design you're interested in. Also look at their previous projects to assess the quality of their work.
2.  Style: Every designer has their own style. Make sure the designer's style matches what you're looking for in your project. Look at examples of their work to see whether they meet your design expectations.
3.  Availability: Check whether the designer is available to work on your project within a timeframe that suits you. Make sure they can devote enough time and attention to your project to achieve the best possible results.
