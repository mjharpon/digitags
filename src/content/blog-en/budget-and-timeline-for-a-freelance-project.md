# **How to set the budget and timeline for a project that needs freelancers**

You have a project in mind and you're ready to put together a team of freelancers to see it through? That's great! But how do you pull it off? How do you set the budget and timeline needed to build this team and carry your project successfully to completion? In this article, we'll cover three key aspects: shaping a project, managing the project budget and setting the timeline. We'll then look at the tools available, along with a few techniques that can help you get the best possible result. Ready to start your journey to success? Then let's go!

## **Preparing the project**

Preparation is the key phase for succeeding with a project involving freelancers. A number of steps need to be followed to make sure the work can be completed successfully and on time.

Good organisation is essential when managing a team of freelancers.

### **Set the project's objectives and expectations**

It's essential to clearly define the project's objectives and expectations before you start looking for freelancers. Determine precisely what is expected in terms of results and which services or products you'll deliver, while staying within the allotted budget and timeline.

### **Define each team member's roles and responsibilities**

You'll need to decide who will be the project's main coordinator, the [project manager](/en/roles/freelance-project-manager), and which other members will make up the team. Specific roles must be clearly defined so that everyone knows exactly what is expected of them.

### **Identify the resources needed for the project**

Once you've defined the objectives and formed a team, you'll need to identify and allocate all the resources you need to carry out your project successfully. This includes not only people, but also additional tools and technologies, as well as external services if necessary.

### **Draw up a schedule and a budget for the project**

The schedule and the budget are arguably the most important project management tools. The schedule should include the various stages of the project, such as planning, coding, the test suite and so on, along with the estimated duration of each stage. Likewise, the budget must cover all staffing (freelance) costs and the other costs associated with the project.

### **Determine the tools and technologies needed for the project**

The right technology tools can save a team a lot of time while managing a freelance project. These tools include, for example, web systems for team communication and generating reports; CRM solutions for managing leads and customers; security systems for protecting websites; external storage services, and so on.

## **Managing the timeline and budget**

To successfully deliver a project involving freelancers, it's important to manage the timeline and budget well. Good management enables the team to meet deadlines and reach the objectives set, within budget.

### **Assess the project's costs and timeline**

Even before you start bringing in freelancers, you'll need to accurately assess the total cost of the project as well as the time needed to complete it. This includes not only the freelancers' fees, but also all the other costs associated with the project, such as those related to hosting, external services or the technology tools required.

### **Assess the project's risks**

Every project carries a certain level of risk. It's therefore necessary to identify these risks before engaging freelancers.

Risks may include late delivery, insufficient quality, incompatibility between different tools or an unexpected increase in costs.

### **Establish procedures for managing the timeline and budget**

Once you've identified the main risks associated with the project, you can put clear and effective procedures in place to ensure your project is delivered on time and within budget. These procedures may include producing weekly or monthly progress reports on the project, using specialised software to generate these reports automatically, or holding regular meetings between the company and its freelance collaborators.

### **Determine how the timeline and budget will be monitored**

It's essential to have a simple and effective way of monitoring and keeping track of the project's schedule and budget in real time. Flexible software that lets you follow each phase of development is strongly recommended, so you have complete visibility over the project's progress.

### **Determine the channels of communication between the team and the client**

Communication is essential to make a freelance project a success. It's therefore important for the team and the client to be in regular contact to make sure all objectives are met on time and to the agreed standards.

Communication tools such as Skype, Slack or live meetings can be very useful for communicating and resolving any issues.

## **Working with freelancers**

Once you've prepared your project and put a plan in place for managing the timeline and budget, you'll need to bring in freelancers to complete each phase of the project. You'll need to take a variety of factors into account before choosing who to work with.

### **Identify the freelancers who match the project's needs**

You'll need to find freelancers whose skills exactly match the specific needs of your project. The best way to go about it is to use platforms like dgitags.io, which can bring you real expertise in defining the ideal candidate and also quickly give you a selection of available, qualified profiles; or to review a large number of candidates and then make a short final selection based on the qualifications, experience and specialisms required.

### **Define the freelancers' terms and conditions of work**

Once you've found your freelance collaborators, you'll need to clearly set out the terms and conditions that will apply to them throughout the process. Points to include are, among others, the total amount to be paid, the delivery deadline, the use of copyright, and so on.

### **Assess the freelancers' skills and experience**

Before engaging a freelancer, it's important to carry out a thorough review of their professional and technical background to make sure they have the right qualifications to complete the project successfully. It's advisable to ask for professional references so you can compare different candidates more easily.

In conclusion, setting the budget and timeline for a project that needs freelancers is a crucial exercise for ensuring the project's success. It's important to take into account the freelancers' costs, delivery deadlines, the project's needs and the skills and experience of the professionals involved. Careful planning will ensure good communication between the parties involved and minimise the risk of budget overruns and late delivery. By following these steps, you'll be able to build a solid plan that lets you carry your project through successfully with a team of skilled and reliable freelancers.
