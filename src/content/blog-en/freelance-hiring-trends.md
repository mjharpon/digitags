Trends in freelance hiring are evolving fast, and it's essential for companies to stay on top of these changes. Whether you're a fast-growing start-up or a large company, understanding the latest trends in [**hiring freelancers**](/en/hire-a-freelancer) can make all the difference to the way you build your team.

The independent work market is growing exponentially, giving you access to a wealth of highly qualified talent specialising in a wide range of fields.

In this article, we explore the latest trends in freelance hiring and give you tips on how to build them into your own talent strategy.

## **The rise of the independent economy**

More and more professionals are choosing independent work, preferring the flexibility and autonomy it offers. This trend is having a significant impact on freelance hiring, as companies look to adapt to this new reality.

One of the main factors behind the rise of the independent economy is the evolution of communication technologies. With the advent of the internet, it has become easier for freelancers to find opportunities and for companies to bring them on board. This increased accessibility has allowed the independent work market to expand rapidly.

Freelancers are also drawn by the advantages this way of working offers. They enjoy greater freedom in managing their schedule, can choose the projects that interest them and often have the option of working remotely. This flexibility attracts a great deal of talent and fuels the growth of the independent economy.

## **The benefits of hiring freelancers**

Hiring freelancers offers companies many benefits. First of all, it means access to a highly qualified, specialised workforce without having to take on full-time staff. Freelancers can bring specific expertise to a given project, which can be extremely beneficial for companies that need specialist skills in the short or medium term.

Hiring freelancers lets companies reduce their labour costs. Freelancers are often less expensive to engage than full-time staff, as they don't require the associated benefits and costs. This lets companies optimise their budget while getting quality work.

Another advantage of hiring freelancers is the flexibility it gives companies. Freelancers can be engaged for specific projects, letting companies adjust their headcount to current needs. This is particularly useful for companies whose business is subject to seasonal fluctuations. Find out in more detail why you might [**hire a freelancer rather than on a permanent contract (CDI)**](/en/blog/freelancer-vs-permanent-hire).

## **Trends in freelance hiring**

According to a report published by *URSSAF* (France's social security contributions agency) in 2020, the number of freelancers rose by 10.4% in just one year, from 2019 to 2020, and the figure keeps climbing. The data also shows a significant increase in the number of tenders for complex projects seeking qualified freelancers.

The trend continues towards greater use of freelance talent, with growing demand for specialist services such as digital marketing, design, development... What's more, the percentage of freelancers relative to salaried staff appears to be rising, as they are more flexible and make it possible to change the make-up of teams quickly as needs evolve.

Companies have also raised their skill requirements. Clients are more often looking for freelancers with solid experience in design, development and software engineering, which underlines the growing importance of the work carried out by independent professionals.

Freelance hiring has evolved in recent years, and new trends have emerged. One of the most striking is the growing use of freelance platforms. These platforms make it easier to find and hire freelancers by connecting companies with professionals suited to their needs.

More and more companies are also turning to recommendations and referrals from existing freelancers to find new collaborators. Recommendations are an effective way of finding reliable, qualified freelancers, because they're based on other professionals' first-hand experience.

## **The most in-demand freelance skills**

The most in-demand freelance skills vary depending on market needs. However, some skills are generally highly sought after. Here are a few examples of skills currently in strong demand among freelancers:

1.  [**Web**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer) and mobile development: with the growth of e-commerce and mobile apps, web and mobile development skills are in high demand. Freelancers specialising in programming languages such as HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python and Java are highly sought after.
2.  Design: companies are increasingly looking to stand out visually, which has led to strong demand for freelancers specialising in design. Skills in logo creation, user interface design and image retouching are particularly sought after.
3.  Content writing: demand for quality content is constantly increasing, which has created strong demand for freelancers specialising in content writing, particularly [**SEO**](/en/roles/freelance-seo-consultant). Skills in copywriting, search engine optimisation and blog writing are highly sought after.

## **The challenges of freelance hiring and how to overcome them**

Freelance hiring presents some unique challenges that companies need to tackle. Here are a few of them, with tips on how to overcome them:

1.  Managing trust and communication: when working with freelancers, it's essential to build a relationship of trust and maintain clear communication. Set clear expectations from the outset and make sure you communicate regularly with the freelancer so the project moves in the right direction. At digitags, we handle project follow-up and act as a trusted partner, both for the client and for the freelance talent.
2.  Finding reliable freelancers: it can be hard to find reliable freelancers who meet deadlines and deliver quality work. To minimise this risk, look for freelancers with good ratings and positive feedback from previous clients. You can also ask for references or run skills tests to assess a freelancer's reliability.
3.  Managing remote coordination: working with freelancers often means working remotely, which can present coordination challenges. Use online [**project management tools**](/en/blog/best-tools-to-collaborate-with-freelancers) to make collaboration and communication between you and the freelancer easier. Schedule regular meetings to discuss the project's progress and resolve any issues.

## **Tips for optimising freelance hiring**

Freelance hiring offers companies many opportunities to find highly qualified talent and benefit from the flexibility and expertise they bring. By understanding the latest trends in freelance hiring and following best practice, you'll be able to make the most of this way of working.

By working closely with freelancers, setting clear expectations and keeping communication open, you'll be able to optimise your results and get the most out of hiring freelancers.

Don't forget to value your freelancers' work and maintain a positive relationship with them. By showing respect and appreciation, you'll be able to [**retain the best talent**](/en/blog/attract-and-retain-top-freelance-talent) and build long-term partnerships.
