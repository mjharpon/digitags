Artificial intelligence. You hear the term everywhere — today it's in your phone, in your fridge... and above all, in your website! If you've blinked for more than 30 seconds over the past decade, you may have missed the tidal wave that AI represents in web development.

So, don't panic. Get comfortable (with a coffee, or a cat on your lap, or both), because we're going to dive together into the current AI trends in web development.

## AI in web development: what's that all about?

Imagine your website being able to:

-   understand what your visitors want,
-   respond to them intelligently
-   suggest things they like, before they even know it.

Welcome to the world of AI applied to the web!

AI here means a mix of technologies such as _machine learning_, natural language processing (aka understanding what we write like a real human), and recommendation systems (thanks, Netflix, for the series we didn't want to watch but binge-watched anyway).

## Why is everyone getting into it? The benefits of AI (spoiler: there are plenty)

### 1\. A perfectly polished UX

Thanks to AI, every visitor feels as though the site was designed just for them. Recommended products, tailored content, targeted promotions... it's a bit as if Amazon could read your mind.

### 2\. A crazy amount of time saved for devs

Goodbye to the mind-numbingly tedious tasks (testing, maintenance, repetitive fixes): AI tools can automate the lot. You? You can code with a virgin mojito in hand (or nearly).

### 3\. Laser-sharp data analysis

Gone are the days when you had to guess what your visitors wanted. AI analyses their behaviour, spots trends and serves you the insights on a plate.

## AI trends to watch in 2025

### Chatbots, your new 24/7 colleagues

They never sleep. They don't take smoke breaks. And they can handle thousands of customers at once. AI chatbots are real Swiss Army knives for your customer support.

### Real-time personalisation

Thanks to AI, your site adapts in real time to your users' (sometimes fickle) desires. They like red trainers? BAM. Here's a promotion right on target. They're hesitating? We show them a glowing customer review.

### Predictive analytics

Your site already knows what your users will like tomorrow. Yes, really. By analysing their past habits, it can predict their next steps, like a digital psychic. Handy for anticipating stock, campaigns or even bugs!

## But beware: it's not all rosy in the world of AI

### 1\. Data is gold… but it's also sensitive

Using AI means collecting data. And data is sacred. GDPR, consent, secure storage... this is no laughing matter.

### 2\. AI can get it wrong

If it's poorly trained, AI can draw the wrong conclusions. Translation? You end up offering pushchairs to teenagers.

### 3\. Algorithmic bias

Yes indeed, AI can have prejudices. Not deliberately, but if the training data is biased, the results will be too. Hence the importance of watching your algorithms like a hawk.
