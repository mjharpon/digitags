Looking to integrate freelancers into your existing team? Bringing external workers into a team can be a challenge. Finding the right blend of skills, expertise and value is essential to creating the environment for effective, harmonious work. In this article, we share practical tips to help you integrate them properly.

## **Define your team's needs**

Before bringing in a freelancer, take the time to clearly define your team's needs, how the freelancer could answer them and how this additional member can help reach your goals. Once you've identified that, you'll be able to properly define the mission profile and prepare for the freelancer's arrival.

Freelancers can bring a wide variety of skills. Identify the technical skills, the industry-specific knowledge and the interpersonal skills needed for a harmonious integration into your team.

## **How do you find the best freelancers?**

The simplest way to find qualified freelancers is to use specialised online networks. Many let you post your brief and find freelancers matching your criteria.

When searching for freelancers, make sure to carefully review their profile and their ratings. Also check their references and past experience to confirm they have the skills to carry your project through.

## **The steps to integrate freelancers into your team**

Here's a memo gathering the steps to tick off for a successful integration:

1.  Clearly define the roles and responsibilities: Setting clear expectations for freelancers and defining their roles and responsibilities within the team is essential. It prevents role conflicts and misunderstandings.
2.  Hold integration meetings: Plan introduction meetings to present the freelancers to the existing team. It helps the freelancers better understand how your team operates and feel more at ease.
3.  Make communication easy: Set up effective communication channels so freelancers stay in touch with the team. Use online tools such as Slack or Microsoft Teams to foster collaboration and coordination. To go further, see our article on [**the best tools for collaborating with freelancers**](/en/blog/best-tools-to-collaborate-with-freelancers).
4.  Set clear objectives: Define clear objectives for the freelancers so they know what's expected of them. It helps them focus on the priority tasks and contribute effectively to the project.

If you want to do things perfectly, you can even invite the new member to the team's social activities (team building) so they can fully join the informal conversations that boost collective motivation.

By following these steps, you should avoid the big mistakes and ensure a smooth integration.

## **Conclusion: freelancers as an asset for your existing team**

By following this advice, you'll be able to effectively integrate a freelancer into your existing team — which can not only improve the team's performance, but also ease innovation and creativity.

Integrating freelancers into an existing team brings many advantages. For a successful integration, it's essential to clearly define roles and responsibilities, communicate transparently and regularly, adapt existing processes to the freelancers' needs and foster a collaborative team culture. Remember to provide the resources and tools freelancers need to work effectively. Follow this advice and you'll successfully integrate freelancers into your existing team, raise your productivity and reach your business goals.
