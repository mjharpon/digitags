## Why use a freelance platform?

Companies are increasingly turning to **freelance platforms**. Here are the four main reasons:

### 1\. A pool of skills

No more headaches finding freelancers. ### 2\. Simplified processes

Contracts, follow-up, invoices, secure payments: everything is centralised, so you stress less. Win-win.

### 3\. Total flexibility

Need a [Google Ads](/en/roles/freelance-google-ads-expert) expert for two months? An illustrator for three social media visuals? On-demand is the new permanent contract (CDI).

### 4\. Budget control

You pay for a deliverable. Costs are calibrated, measured, optimised… in short, your CFO is smiling.

## Malt: XXL volume

With more than 600,000 freelancers, Malt is the hypermarket of **freelance platforms**. You'll find everything there: from [full-stack](/en/roles/freelance-full-stack-developer) devs to 3D motion designers, complete with a snack aisle and self-checkouts.

### Advantages

-   **Large pool** of tech, creative and marketing profiles.
-   **Quick**, transparent **quotes**.
-   TripAdvisor-style **ratings & reviews**: handy for filtering.

### Drawbacks

-   Less rigorous selection: the CV sorting is down to you.
-   Responsive but generic support: no dedicated [project manager](/en/roles/freelance-project-manager).

## Upwork: the international option

Present in 180 countries, Upwork brings together talent from all over the world.

### Advantages

-   **Varied rates**: from beginners to senior gurus.
-   **Hourly tracking**: the log-time tool that counts every minute (and every coffee).

### Drawbacks

-   **Time zones**: your 9 am daily stand-up might land at 3 am for them.
-   **Fierce competition**: 50 applications in 24 hours. Your brief turns into a Battle Royale.
