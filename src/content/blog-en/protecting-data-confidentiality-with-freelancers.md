# **Protecting your company's data confidentiality when working with freelancers**

Freelancers have quickly established themselves as key players in the professional world. As companies face a shortage of qualified talent, they can count on freelancers to provide specialist work and deliver viable strategic services. However, when it comes to protecting your company's data confidentiality, it's essential to take certain measures. In this article, we'll explain how to set up a freelance contract with a non-compete clause to guarantee the protection of the crucial, sensitive information freelancers need access to.

## **Legal obligations**

When a client company and a freelancer agree on the provision of a service, they must conclude a contract. This contract can take the form of a simple work contract or a more complex contract including additional clauses.

Whatever type of contract is chosen, the rights and obligations of the parties are clearly defined.

### **What legal contracts exist between a client company and a freelancer?**

A contract is the legal basis on which any contractual relationship between a client company and a freelancer rests. It is a written document that defines and specifies the rights and obligations of each party.

A work contract is often needed to provide a legal framework for this relationship. In France, there are different forms of work contract: fixed-term contract (*CDD*, *Contrat à Durée Déterminée*), permanent contract (*CDI*, *Contrat à Durée Indéterminée*), etc. 

### **What are the rights and obligations of both parties?**

The rights and obligations of the freelancer and the client generally appear in the contract, but some are also imposed by the French Labour Code (*code du travail*) or any other regulations applicable in the country concerned. The freelancer has the right to demand reasonable pay for their work, compensation if certain contractual conditions are not met, and protection against potential abuse by the client regarding the content of the contract.

On the other hand, the client has the right to obtain the product or service requested through the contract and requires that these be delivered in the best possible conditions according to the agreed terms.

### **Which confidentiality clauses should you include in a freelance contract?**

A confidentiality clause is very important to protect privileged or confidential information exchanged between the client company and the freelancer during the engagement. This clause can prohibit the freelancer from disclosing any information about the company that could harm its reputation or compromise its competitiveness.

It can also cover any document, commercial or personal information whose disclosure could damage the company or the freelancer. It is essential that this clause is clearly defined and that both parties accept it. It must absolutely provide for a penalty in the event of a breach, set out in writing in the contract. You can also include clauses relating to unfair competition, non-solicitation and a ban on using professional contacts for personal purposes.

## **Information protection measures**

Any company that engages a freelancer must take measures to protect its confidential information, whether personal or commercial data. These measures mainly consist of putting systems and procedures in place so that the freelancer cannot access sensitive information.

### **How do you protect confidential information?**

Companies can put various means in place to secure their data in the context of a contractual relationship with a freelancer. For example, they can:

-   Restrict physical access to premises and servers;
-   Protect IT networks against intrusion;
-   Restrict access through usernames and passwords;
-   Encrypt data;
-   Organise regular operating system updates.

### **What are the means of securing data?**

Once the technical systems have been put in place, you need to make sure that all members of an organisation respect data protection principles. This starts with training every staff member and freelancer on confidentiality and the handling of the company's sensitive data.

An internal charter is often essential as a reminder of the good practices to follow. There are also solutions such as electronic signatures that make it possible to legally seal a transaction.

### **How do you ensure confidentiality is respected?**

To guarantee that confidentiality is properly respected, it's important to include this clause in the initial contract signed between the client company and the freelancer. In addition, internal checks (IT audits) should be carried out regularly to verify that the clause is being applied.

## **Legal consequences**

Breaching confidentiality clauses or the GDPR is punishable by law. Indeed, the penalties applicable to those responsible are very severe and can range from a fine to a prison sentence in the most serious cases. 

### **What are the legal consequences of a confidentiality breach?**

In the event of a breach of the contractual clauses relating to confidentiality, the freelancer is liable to a criminal penalty such as a fine and/or prison. It is therefore important to be well informed about the legal obligations and to take the necessary measures to respect these clauses.

Moreover, if the freelancer uses or discloses confidential information for commercial or other purposes, they can be prosecuted for unfair competition or anti-competitive practices.

### **What remedies are available to the client and the freelancer?**

The client and the freelancer each have various means of asserting their rights if the confidentiality clause is not respected. In particular, the client can turn to a competent court to seek compensation if their company has suffered direct harm as a result of the breach.

### **How do you avoid conflicts and disputes related to confidentiality?**

To avoid any misunderstanding between the freelancer and the company, the first thing to do is to clearly define the legal framework guaranteeing strict compliance with contractual obligations. Once this framework is in place, it is advisable to hold regular meetings between the client company and the freelancer to remind each party of the commitments they have made.

In conclusion, protecting your company's data confidentiality is essential when working with freelancers. The risks of data breaches and theft of confidential information can be significant if appropriate security measures are not taken. It is therefore crucial to make sure that the freelancers you work with understand the importance of data confidentiality and that they respect your company's security policies. It is also recommended to put security controls in place, such as restricting access to data, using encryption software and implementing password management policies. By protecting the confidentiality of your data, you can minimise the risks to your company and secure the trust of your clients and business partners.
