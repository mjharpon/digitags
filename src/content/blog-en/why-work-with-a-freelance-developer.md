You have a web project in mind, but the idea of hiring a full-time developer brings you out in a cold sweat? Don't panic—the answer might well be to work with a [**freelance developer**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer): flexible, expert and often more cost-effective.

## Why bring in a freelance developer?

### Sharp expertise within reach

Freelance developers are often specialists in their field. Whether it's JavaScript, Python or more exotic frameworks, these experts are used to diving into varied projects and adapting quickly. Their experience allows them to propose innovative, effective solutions, even for the toughest challenges.

### Flexibility and responsiveness: the winning combo

Need a hand with an urgent project or a specific skill for a one-off engagement? That's exactly what a freelance developer is for. Without the constraints of a long-term contract, you can adjust resources to your needs. And the icing on the cake: freelancers are often used to working unusual hours, which can come in very handy for tight deadlines.

### A smart economic choice

Taking on a salaried staff member means fixed costs: salaries, social charges, various benefits... With a freelancer, you only pay for the work done. No hidden costs, no surprises. That lets you keep control of your budget while benefiting from top-level expertise. Read our article on [**why hire a freelancer rather than a permanent contract (CDI)**](/en/blog/freelancer-vs-permanent-hire).

### Access to a worldwide talent pool

Thanks to online platforms and modern communication tools, it's now easy to collaborate with freelance developers all over the world. This openness gives you access to varied skills and different perspectives, enriching your projects.

### A rewarding collaboration

Working with a freelancer is also an opportunity to discover new ways of working, to learn and to enrich one another. Freelancers often bring a fresh eye and new ideas, which can be very beneficial for your company.
