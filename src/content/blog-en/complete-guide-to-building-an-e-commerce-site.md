## **What makes an e-commerce site different**

An e-commerce site isn't designed like a brochure website or a platform: here we need to be able to sell products or services directly to consumers. Instead of, or alongside, a physical shop, an e-commerce site lets you reach an international audience and operate 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Users can browse offers, place orders and make payments from their sofa.

An e-commerce site is generally made up of several key elements: a product catalogue, an order management system and secure payment tools. It's also important to consider the user experience, as a well-designed site can influence customers' buying behaviour. Building an e-commerce site also means complying with certain rules, particularly those concerning data protection and transaction security.

An e-commerce site isn't just a question of technology. It also requires an in-depth understanding of the market, target customers and marketing strategies. That's why [**running a UX research phase**](/en/projects/ux-research) can be a good idea before getting started.

## **Online shopping statistics**

Statistics show that online commerce continues to grow at a healthy pace. According to recent studies, the global e-commerce market is expected to reach several trillion dollars over the next few years. Many consumers now prefer to shop online rather than go to a physical shop. The total number of online transactions reached 2.3 billion in 2024, an increase of 8% compared with 2023.

In France, the figures are also encouraging: a study found that nearly 80% of internet users made an online purchase over the past year. In 2024, that's 39.4 million French people who shopped online, 500,000 more than in 2023. The most popular online categories include fashion/clothing, footwear and hygiene/beauty products.

It's worth noting that online customer loyalty is also on the right track, and that's the crux of the battle for e-merchants. Customers who have a positive shopping experience are more likely to come back and recommend the brand to others. Stats indicate that a loyal customer can spend up to five times more than a new one.

## **Essential features of an e-commerce site**

For an e-commerce site to perform, it must include certain must-have features. An intuitive user interface is paramount. Visitors must be able to navigate the site easily, find the products they're looking for and make their purchases without obstacles. So you need an effective search bar, filters to refine results and a clear product presentation.

Transaction security is an absolute priority. Make sure your site uses the HTTPS protocol to guarantee the security of sensitive data. Integrating secure payment methods such as PayPal, Stripe or traditional banking solutions is also essential to reassure your customers when they buy.

Finally, analytics and reporting tools are essential for tracking your site's performance. Features such as conversion tracking, user behaviour analysis and sales reports will help you understand what works and what needs to be improved or changed. By using this data, you'll be able to adjust your marketing strategies and optimise the customer experience.
