Do you want to create a website for your business, or redesign an existing one? You have several options: build it yourself if you have the skills, work with a freelancer, or call on a development agency. You want to be sure you find an agency that understands your needs and your brand, and that can create a website that will attract visitors and encourage them to take action.

In this article, I share some valuable advice to help you choose the best web design agency. You'll learn how to assess their technical skills, their experience in your sector, their portfolio and their client references.

## **Why create a website for your business?**

If you've landed on this article, you're probably already convinced: having a website is essential today. A well-designed website can help you stand out from the competition, attract new customers and strengthen your online presence. Here are some of the main benefits of having a website for your business:

1.  Online presence: A website gives your business an online presence 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. This means your potential customers can find you and learn more about your products and services at any time.
2.  Lead generation: A website can be a powerful tool for generating leads and sales. By using contact forms, CTAs or e-commerce features, you can encourage visitors to your site to take action, request a quote or make a purchase.
3.  Credibility: Having a professional website can give your business more credibility. Your customers expect you to have an online presence, and a well-designed website is a sign of professionalism and reliability.

## **The different types of web design agencies**

When you're looking for an agency to build your website, it's important to understand the different types of agencies available. Here are some of the most common:

1.  Freelance collectives: These groups of independent designers and developers work like a studio, project by project. They can offer web design services at a more affordable cost, and can take on complex projects.
2.  Traditional web design agencies: These agencies offer a full range of services, from initial design through to implementation and ongoing maintenance. They often have a multidisciplinary team of designers, developers, marketing specialists and content professionals.
3.  Offshore web design agencies: These agencies are based in other countries and offer web design services at a cost that is often significantly lower. They can be an attractive option for businesses looking for budget-friendly solutions, but it's important to take linguistic and cultural differences into account.

## **How do you define your website needs?**

Before choosing your agency, it's essential to clearly define your website objectives and needs. Here are a few questions to consider:

1.  What is the main goal of your website? Do you want to sell products online, promote your services or simply provide information to your visitors?
2.  What specific features do you want to include on your website? For example, do you need an online booking system, a contact form, a blog or an online shop?
3.  Who is your target audience? What are your target audience's needs and preferences when it comes to web design? Discover [**the 6 web design trends in our latest article.**](/en/blog/6-web-design-trends-2024)
4.  Have you already defined a brand identity: the use of specific colours, typefaces or layouts?
5.  Do you have any preferences regarding the language or CMS used to build your site? Do you prefer no-code?
6.  Do you need to hand over the website's maintenance for the long term?

In an ideal world, and to frame your project properly — especially if it's complex — you'll write a complete and precise functional specification upfront.

By answering these questions, you'll be better able to communicate your needs and expectations to the web design agency, which will make the selection process easier and ensure the agency you choose is able to meet your specific needs.
