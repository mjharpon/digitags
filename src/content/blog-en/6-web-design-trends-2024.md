Trends come and go, so I'm sharing the 6 web design trends that caught my eye this year. Between minimalist design, micro-interactions and AI, there's plenty to talk about! This article sums up the trend watch I carried out over the first 6 months of the year, and some of these trends can also be applied to other marketing activities, so I strongly recommend reading through them even if you're not planning a website redesign.

## **Trend 1: Minimalism and simplicity**

The trend towards minimalism and simplicity is stronger than ever in 2024. Designers are looking to strip out superfluous elements to create clean, functional websites. This approach lets users focus on what matters and navigate the site more easily.

You'll find neutral colours, white space and minimalist typography that convey a feeling of calm and clarity. Minimalist websites also load faster, which improves the user experience. To learn more about the importance of your site's loading time, I recommend the article on [**technical SEO**](https://donutzdigital.com/fr/le-guide-ultime-du-seo-technique/) by [**Donutz Digital**](https://donutzdigital.com/fr/), our digital marketing agency.

Minimalism doesn't mean websites are boring or lack personality, though. On the contrary, images and illustrations are used strategically to add visual interest without overloading the page.

Here's [**an example of a minimalist site**](https://studiod.nu/en/).

## **Trend 2: Using virtual and augmented reality**

Virtual reality (VR) and augmented reality (AR) continue to play an important role in web design in 2024. These technologies offer immersive, interactive possibilities for mobile users, creating more engaging online experiences. Websites use virtual reality to let users visit virtual places, try out products or take part in interactive activities. Augmented reality, for its part, lets users overlay virtual elements onto the real world, offering a unique and innovative experience.

Using virtual and augmented reality in web design requires close collaboration between designers, developers and marketing specialists. Creating immersive experiences that match the brand's objectives and offer genuine added value to users isn't that simple.

The IKEA app, for example, lets you see what furniture would look like in your home.

## **Trend 3: Animations and micro-interactions**

These subtle little animations and intuitive interactions bring websites to life, making them more dynamic and engaging. Users appreciate animations that enhance their browsing experience, but it's important to strike the right balance. Animations should be used strategically to avoid distracting or irritating users.

Micro-interactions, meanwhile, are small animations or reactions in response to the user's actions. For example, a button that changes colour on hover or an icon that moves slightly when clicked. These micro-interactions add a touch of interactivity to websites.

Here's [**an example of a site**](https://www.sugar.health/) that uses micro-interactions and animations, and in my opinion it's really well done.

## **Trend 4: Bold typography**

Designers have turned to bolder, more expressive typefaces to give websites character and impact. Custom, unique typefaces are also popular, allowing brands to stand out and strengthen their visual identity.

Bold typography can be used to highlight headings, subheadings, calls to action and other important elements on the website. It's important to strike the right balance between legibility and aesthetics when using original typefaces. Too much is often the enemy of good.

Here's [**an example of a site**](https://thickpickle.com/) that I love and that brings together almost all the trends in this article.

## **Trend 5: Integrating video and audio**

It had been a long time since I'd seen websites with audio, and yet in 2024 this trend has come back into the spotlight. Video has the advantage of capturing users' attention and conveying information in a more engaging way than text alone.

Audio strengthens user interactions and adds a truly immersive feel to the experience. The only downside: it's important to take loading speed into account when integrating video and audio into a website.

Here's [**an example of a site**](https://spaace.io/) that uses it beautifully.

## **Trend 6: Use of colour**

Just like typefaces, bold, vivid colour palettes are back, grabbing users' attention and creating visually striking designs. Colour gradients and contrasting colour combinations are also popular for adding depth and dimension to websites.

It's important to choose colours that match the brand's identity and values. Colours can influence users' mood and emotions, so it's essential to choose them wisely to create a consistent and engaging experience.

It's also important to consider accessibility when choosing colours. Make sure contrasts are sufficient for users with impaired vision, and use captions or alternative descriptions for visually impaired users.

Here's [**an example of a site**](https://astroshock.com/).

## **Conclusion**

In 2024, the web design landscape continues to evolve rapidly, offering new possibilities and technologies for creating captivating online experiences. This article summed up my favourite trends. I'm sure the end of the year has new trends in store for us. By adapting your strategy to include these trends, you'll be able to offer an unforgettable user experience and stand out from the competition.
