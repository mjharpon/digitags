Finding the right freelancer for your project isn't always easy. Between CVs that all look the same, availability that never lines up and skills that are hard to evaluate, finding a freelancer can quickly turn into an obstacle course. At digitags.work, we decided to radically simplify the process. Our mission? Connecting you with the independent talent that genuinely matches your needs — no wasted time, no nasty surprises. In this article, we take you behind the scenes of our matching method and show why it makes all the difference.

## Why the classic way of finding freelancers is broken

Let's be honest: most freelance platforms run on a model as old as the web itself. You post a listing, you receive 50 applications of which 45 are completely off-topic, and you spend hours sorting, comparing, evaluating. The result? You lose precious time you could spend on something else.

The problem isn't the freelancers themselves. It's the lack of qualification upstream. When a company looks for a React developer with 5 years of e-commerce experience and receives applications from junior PHP developers or graphic designers, there's a serious bug in the system.

Another classic pitfall: availability. How many times have you identified the perfect profile, only to discover they're booked until the end of the year? Or they accept your mission but juggle three other clients and deliver rushed work?

Not to mention the administrative headache: invoicing, contracts, legal statuses, insurance… Bringing in a freelancer often means improvising as an expert in labour law and admin management. Not exactly your cup of tea when you run a small business or you're a CTO with 1,000 priorities.

## Our philosophy: quality before quantity

At digitags.work, we made the radical choice of relevance over volume. Our freelance matching process doesn't aim to drown you in profiles, but to present only the talent that ticks your boxes. Three ultra-qualified candidates beat fifty approximate profiles.

This approach rests on a simple conviction: your time is precious. You have neither the desire nor the resources to become a full-time recruiter. You want an available, competent, reliable expert who starts quickly and delivers results. Full stop.

Our role? Playing translator between your business needs and freelancers' technical skills. Because a small-business founder doesn't necessarily speak the same language as a [full-stack developer](/en/roles/freelance-full-stack-developer) or a growth hacker. We decode, clarify and refine until the brief is perfectly understood on both sides.

## The 5 stages of our matching process

### Stage 1: Genuinely understanding your need

Everything starts with an in-depth conversation with you — a real human exchange. We dig into your project, your constraints, your business goals. What's the expected deliverable? What level of expertise is required? What are your realistic deadlines? What budget have you allocated?

We also ask questions you may not have asked yourself. Will the freelancer need to integrate into an existing team? Will they work fully remote, or will on-site sessions be needed? Do they need access to specific tools or sensitive data?

This discovery phase lets us avoid 90% of casting mistakes. Because a well-crafted brief is already half the way to a successful freelance engagement.

### Stage 2: Targeted sourcing in our qualified pool

We don't launch a public call for applications. Instead, we dive into our base of 1,600+ pre-qualified freelancers we're used to working with. We know their real skills, their areas of expertise, their mission history, their strengths and their watch-points.

We cross-reference a dozen criteria: technical skills of course, but also soft skills, availability, budget fit, location when it matters, industry experience… We don't just match keywords; we analyse the overall compatibility between your project and the freelancer's profile.

This stage can take from a few hours to a few days depending on how specific your request is. But you save weeks compared with a classic recruitment process.

### Stage 3: Shortlisting and field verification

The profiles that come out of our first filter aren't sent straight to your inbox. We take the time to contact them one by one to validate their real availability, their interest in your project and their fit with your specific constraints.

That conversation also lets us detect the weak signals. An ultra-competent freelancer already juggling four missions in parallel? We set them aside. A promising talent who has never worked in your industry when that's crucial for you? We dig deeper to gauge their ability to adapt.

We only propose freelancers who are motivated, available and genuinely aligned with your need. Usually between two and four profiles.

### Stage 4: Introductions and support

We organise the first exchanges between you and the selected freelancers. These meetings can take the form of video calls, in-person meetings or even technical tests depending on the nature of the mission.

Our added value here? We stay in the loop to ease communication. We help clarify grey areas, align mutual expectations and negotiate terms in a win-win spirit. Because a successful freelance collaboration is, above all, a relationship of trust that starts on solid ground.

If none of the presented profiles fully convinces you, we go back to sourcing — always at no cost. Your satisfaction is the only KPI that counts.

### Stage 5: A smooth start and mission follow-up

Once the freelancer is chosen, we don't vanish into thin air. We support you through the start of the collaboration: detailed mission scoping, contracts, tool access, initial briefing…

Throughout the mission, we stay available whenever needed. A communication problem? A disagreement on deliverables? An unexpected event that calls for a scope adjustment? We step in to unblock the situation quickly.

This approach is what lets us maintain an excellent client satisfaction rate and a very high mission renewal rate.

## What makes our matching different

### Real business understanding

We don't just do keyword matching between a need and a CV. Our team has the domain expertise to understand the technical, business and organisational stakes of your project. We know that a CTO looking for a back-end developer isn't just looking for someone who knows a language, but a profile able to scale an architecture, write clean code and work as part of a team.

### A closed, qualified network

Our freelance pool isn't open to everyone. Every new talent goes through a rigorous validation process: an in-depth interview, reference checks, soft-skills evaluation. The result? We only work with the best freelancers we've found in each field.

### Availability verified in real time

Unlike classic platforms where profiles often display fanciful availability, we manually verify each freelancer's real capacity to take on your mission. If we present you a profile, it's because they can start within the timeframe you've set.

### Human support from start to finish

No chatbot, no 100% automated process. You have a dedicated contact who knows your file and can answer your questions within minutes. Because finding the right freelancer doesn't happen through algorithms alone — it takes relational intelligence and a healthy dose of common sense.

## The concrete benefits for your company

Saving time is obviously the first advantage. Where a classic freelance search can stretch over 3 to 6 weeks, our method lets you identify the right profile in 3 to 7 days on average.

Reducing risk is the second pillar. By presenting only pre-qualified, verified talent, we drastically limit the risk of a bad match.

Optimising your costs is another significant benefit. A failed engagement is expensive: wasted time, delayed project, squandered budget. Our success rate helps you avoid those setbacks and quickly get a return on your investment.

Finally, you benefit from total flexibility. Need to scale up on a project? We find you reinforcements within days. The mission ended earlier than planned? No long-term commitment. You steer your talent management the way you steer your business: with agility.

## Frequently asked questions about our matching process

### How long does the full matching process take?

On average, count 2 to 4 working days between your initial request and the presentation of the first qualified profiles. For very specific needs or rare profiles, that can stretch a little. If you have an absolute emergency, flag it from the start: we can switch to express mode and present immediately available profiles, even if it means loosening a few secondary criteria.

### What happens if none of the profiles suits me?

No problem. We go back to sourcing with your feedback to refine our search. You tell us what didn't fit with the first profiles (insufficient expertise, mismatched soft skills…) and we adjust our criteria accordingly. This re-sourcing is included in our service — you still pay nothing. We keep going until you find the ideal profile.

### How do you guarantee the quality of the freelancers in your network?

Every freelancer joining our network goes through a multi-stage validation process: an in-depth interview with our team to assess technical and interpersonal skills, reference checks with past clients, and a review of their portfolio or previous projects. We only keep the profiles that demonstrate real professionalism. On top of that, we systematically collect client feedback after each mission, which lets us continuously refine the quality of our pool.

### Can I work with the same freelancer on several successive missions?

Absolutely — it's even encouraged! When you find a freelancer who works well with your team and understands your business, it makes sense to keep them. That continuity benefits everyone: you save time on onboarding, the freelancer already knows your context, and the collaboration is all the more effective.

### What's your business model?

Our remuneration generally works as a commission on the freelancer's rate, or sometimes a flat fee. Concretely, you pay nothing until you've actually engaged a freelancer. No sign-up fees, no monthly subscription, no hidden costs. The first phase of advice and matching is entirely free. You only pay a commission once the collaboration actually starts, and that commission is calculated transparently based on the mission's duration and amount.
