Social networks have become an acquisition channel in their own right — provided you go beyond simply boosting posts. A **freelance social ads expert** designs social advertising campaigns that generate leads and sales, not just impressions.

## What is a social ads expert?

The social ads expert (or SMA, Social Media Advertising) runs paid advertising on social platforms: Meta (Facebook, Instagram), LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, Snapchat or X. Where paid search captures existing demand, social ads create it: they reach your future customers before they even look for you, thanks to finely tuned audience targeting and powerful creative formats.

It's a craft at the crossroads of data and creativity: the campaigns that perform rely as much on audience precision as on creatives tested and refreshed continuously.

## The missions of a freelance social ads expert

-   Paid social strategy: choice of platforms, objectives (awareness, leads, sales), budgets.
-   Audience building: socio-demographic and interest targeting, lookalike audiences, retargeting.
-   Creative direction of the ads: formats (video, carousel, UGC), angles, hooks — and systematic A/B testing.
-   B2B campaigns on LinkedIn: lead generation strategy, white papers, native lead forms.
-   Complete funnels: from discovery to conversion retargeting, with messages tuned to each stage.
-   Tracking and measurement: pixels, conversion APIs, attribution — reliable data despite targeting restrictions.
-   Optimisation and reporting: CPL, ROAS, frequency, creative fatigue, actionable recommendations.

## When to bring in a freelance social ads expert?

-   You want to generate qualified B2B leads (LinkedIn) or B2C sales (Meta, TikTok).
-   Your campaigns have plateaued: saturated audiences, tired creatives, rising costs.
-   You're launching a brand and need to create demand, not just capture it.
-   You want ROI-driven management with readable reporting, without an agency retainer.

## The skills to look for

Mastery of the ad platforms (Meta Business Manager, LinkedIn Campaign Manager, TikTok Ads), a creative culture to brief or produce effective ads, and analytical rigour on tracking and attribution. The best profiles collaborate with the [social media manager](/en/roles/freelance-community-manager), the [Google Ads expert](/en/roles/freelance-google-ads-expert) and the [traffic manager](/en/roles/freelance-traffic-manager) to orchestrate your entire acquisition.
