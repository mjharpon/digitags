Whether you're a start-up looking to grow fast or an established company that wants to stay competitive, agile coaching can be the right way to carry your transformation through.

## **What is agile coaching?**

Agile coaching is an approach designed to help you succeed in your digital transformation. This type of coaching helps teams adopt an agile mindset, roll out agile methodologies such as Scrum, Kanban or Lean, and build leadership and collaboration skills. Agile coaching aims to create an environment that fosters innovation, adaptability and speed of execution.

The agile coach's role is to support teams and companies, guiding them through the adoption of new practices and helping them overcome the obstacles and resistance that come with change.

## **The benefits of agile coaching for your company**

Agile coaching rests on several key principles, the main ones being:

1.  Collaboration and communication : agile coaching encourages collaboration and communication within the team and across the company's teams. It fosters the exchange of ideas, knowledge sharing and better decision-making.
2.  Adaptability and flexibility : agile coaching champions flexibility and fast adaptation to change, letting you respond to shifting market needs and adjust your goals and strategies to new opportunities and constraints.
3.  Continuous learning : a pillar of the Lean methodology, continuous learning encourages teams to experiment, evaluate the results and adapt accordingly. These constant iterations are what drive innovation.
4.  Autonomy and accountability : agile coaching pushes teams to make decisions autonomously and own their actions. It builds the team's engagement and motivation, and develops their ability to be solution-oriented rather than problem-oriented.

## **The stages of an agile coaching engagement**

This type of coaching requires specific planning and execution. Here are the key stages of an agile coaching engagement:

1.  Define the vision and the goals : the first stage is defining the vision and goals of the transformation. You give the company a clear direction and rally the teams around a shared objective.
2.  Form an agile team : the second stage is forming an agile team, made up of members from different functions and disciplines. This team will be responsible for the rollout alongside the agile coach.
3.  Set a plan : next comes a detailed roadmap, defining the stages, the resources needed and the delivery timelines.
4.  Roll out agile methodologies : the fourth stage is implementing agile methodologies such as Scrum, Kanban or Lean, mentioned briefly above.
5.  Measure and adjust : for continuous learning, this final stage is central — measure the results and adjust the actions based on the feedback collected.

## **The tools and methodologies used in agile coaching**

Agile coaching draws on a variety of tools and methodologies. A few examples:

1.  Scrum : an agile methodology that lets teams work iteratively and collaboratively. It relies on short development cycles called "sprints" and promotes transparency, accountability and adaptability. It is widely used by [**web development**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer) teams.
2.  Kanban : the Kanban method is based on visualising tasks and managing workflows. It lets teams track projects' progress in real time and make decisions based on concrete data.
3.  Lean : Lean is a methodology that aims to eliminate wasted time and resources and to iterate as quickly and efficiently as possible. It is widely used in start-ups but adapts to every type of company.

## **Agile coaching for the most common challenges and obstacles**

If you're facing any of the following challenges, bringing in an agile coach can be the right move:

1.  Resistance to change : some team members are reluctant to adopt new practices and step out of their comfort zone? The agile coach helps you overcome that resistance by clearly explaining the benefits of the transformation and supporting the team throughout the process.
2.  Skills gaps : the obstacle is a lack of in-house skills? Your people may need to build new skills in technology, leadership and change management. The agile coach identifies the gaps and proposes the right training and resources.
3.  Lack of leadership support : weak support from leadership can derail a digital transformation. The agile coach works closely with leadership to make sure the goals are aligned with the company's vision and the necessary resources are available.
4.  Technological complexity : facing compatibility, integration or security issues? The agile coach works with the technical teams to identify potential problems and propose the right solutions.
