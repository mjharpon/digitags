A freelance data scientist turns your data into decisions and products: forecasting demand, catching fraud, recommending the right content, classifying documents or building an LLM-powered assistant. The job doesn't end in a notebook. A good data scientist asks the right business question, picks the simplest method that works and sees the model all the way into production.

Working with a freelance data scientist gives you sharp expertise exactly when you need it — for a proof of concept, a first model to industrialise or an audit of what you already have.

## **What a freelance data scientist does**

Data scientists sit where statistics, software and business meet. They explore the data available, frame hypotheses, build and evaluate models, then explain the results to people who aren't necessarily technical.

By 2026 the scope has widened. On top of "classic" machine learning — regression, gradient boosting, time series, clustering — come generative AI and RAG architectures, language model evaluation and MLOps. A seasoned freelance data scientist also knows when you don't need a model at all: sometimes a well-defined business rule or a dashboard does the job.

## **Typical missions**

These are the topics our clients most often bring a freelance data scientist in for:

- **Predictive models**: sales or demand forecasting, churn scoring, customer lifetime value, predictive maintenance.
- **Recommendation and personalisation**: product or content recommendation engines, behavioural segmentation, lead prioritisation.
- **Generative AI and LLMs**: internal assistants, semantic search, information extraction from documents — with a rigorous evaluation protocol.
- **Experimentation**: designing and analysing A/B tests, causal inference, measuring the real impact of a feature or campaign.
- **Audit and industrialisation**: reviewing an existing model, rescuing a POC stuck in a notebook, setting up monitoring and retraining.

## **The tools our freelance data scientists use**

The stack depends on your environment, but a few tools show up in almost every mission:

- **Languages and libraries**: Python first (pandas, Polars, scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, PyTorch), plus SQL to query data at the source.
- **Data platforms**: Databricks, Snowflake, BigQuery, and the ML services of the major clouds (Vertex AI, SageMaker, Azure ML).
- **MLOps**: MLflow for experiment tracking and model registry, Docker, CI/CD pipelines, orchestration with Airflow or Dagster.
- **Generative AI**: large language model APIs, open-weight models, vector databases and evaluation frameworks to measure answer quality.

## **Data scientist, data engineer or data analyst?**

The three roles work hand in hand, but they aren't interchangeable. A [**data engineer**](/en/roles/freelance-data-engineer) builds and hardens the pipelines that feed your data warehouse. A [**data analyst**](/en/roles/freelance-data-analyst) uses that data to track performance, build dashboards and answer business questions. The data scientist steps in when you need to predict, automate a decision or make sense of unstructured data.

One piece of advice: if your data is scattered and unreliable, start with data engineering. A model is only ever as good as the data it's trained on — and an experienced freelance data scientist will tell you so at the scoping stage.

## **How a data science mission runs**

A successful mission follows a short, iterative cycle with clear decision points at every stage.

### Scoping

The data scientist turns your business challenge into a problem that can be modelled, defines success metrics and checks that the data exists, is accessible and can be used in line with GDPR.

### Exploration and proof of concept

They analyse the data, set a simple baseline, then try more advanced models. The goal is to show quickly whether the expected gain is within reach — or to stop early if it isn't.

### Industrialisation

The chosen model is packaged, versioned and integrated into your systems with the tech team, alongside a [**back-end developer**](/en/roles/freelance-back-end-developer) or DevOps engineer where needed.

### Monitoring and handover

Performance and data drift monitoring, documentation, handover to your teams: the model has to keep working long after the mission ends.

## **Compliance, ethics and the AI Act**

Models often handle personal data. A freelance data scientist builds GDPR principles in from day one: data minimisation, pseudonymisation, retention periods, a lawful basis for processing. As the EU AI Act gradually comes into force, some use cases — scoring people, high-impact automated decisions — also require documentation, traceability and human oversight. Our freelancers spot these issues early and produce the documentation that goes with them.
