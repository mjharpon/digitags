## **What is a freelance web developer?**

A freelance web developer is an independent expert specialised in building and maintaining websites and applications. Unlike salaried developers working for one company, the freelance web developer works for their own account.

The craft spans many skills: from designing a website's architecture to writing code, through content integration, search engine optimisation (SEO) and application security.

## **The advantages of a freelance web developer for your company**

### **Flexibility and availability of an independent web developer**

One of the key advantages of working with an independent web developer is their flexibility and availability. A freelancer can work closely with you to build or [**redesign a website**](/en/projects/website-redesign) tailored to your specific needs. As independents, they are often more affordable and more flexible on rates and availability — particularly valuable for smaller companies. A freelancer's availability is a major asset too: they can adapt to your schedule and work on urgent projects when needed.

With a freelancer, you can easily adjust your project and make changes when necessary, keeping you agile and responsive to change.

Since most freelance web developers work remotely, you can find them anywhere in the world — widening your talent pool and giving you access to highly qualified, specialised web developers.

### **Cutting costs with a freelance web developer**

One of the main advantages of a freelance web developer is the opportunity to cut costs and save money. Compared with hiring a web development agency, working with a freelancer is often far more cost-effective, especially for smaller companies with limited budgets. Freelancers generally have more flexible rates and can adapt to your specific budget. They can also offer more flexible payment options, such as hourly rates, packages or even fixed-price projects.

## **The skills of a freelance web developer**

First of all, note that there are different types of web developers, the main ones being [front-end](/en/roles/freelance-front-end-developer), back-end, mobile and [full-stack](/en/roles/freelance-full-stack-developer) developers. Skills vary by type, so here are the main ones gathered in one place.

### **Mastery of the languages**

A web developer must master several programming languages. The most common include:

-   **HTML/CSS**: for the structure and base design of websites.
-   **JavaScript**: to add interactive, dynamic features.
-   **PHP, Python, Ruby, Java**: for back-end development.
-   **SQL**: for database management.

Each project may call for a different skill set — we find the talent whose skills match your needs.

### **Knowledge of the tools and frameworks**

Knowing frameworks such as React, Angular and Vue.js for the front end, or Django, Laravel and Node.js for the back end, is a must. Likewise, familiarity with content management systems (CMS) such as WordPress or Webflow is an asset.

### **Project management skills**

Developers are not [**project managers**](/en/roles/freelance-project-manager), but they still need those skills, especially when working as part of a team — with a [**UX designer**](/en/roles/freelance-ux-designer) or [**UI designer**](/en/roles/freelance-ui-designer), for instance. That includes setting deadlines, prioritising tasks and using the right project management tools.
