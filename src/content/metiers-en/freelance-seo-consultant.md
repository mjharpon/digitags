Want to work on your organic search visibility? Meet the freelance SEO consultant. Freelance SEO consultants are technical experts who help you improve your online visibility and drive organic traffic to your website. ## **What does a freelance SEO consultant do?**

A freelance SEO (Search Engine Optimization) consultant is an independent expert in search engine optimisation. They help companies improve their online visibility and generate organic traffic to their website, taking charge of the SEO strategy, keyword research and content optimisation.

Their services typically include:

-   **SEO audit**: they analyse your website to identify what to improve — structure, content, backlinks, loading speed. Bringing in a freelance SEO consultant when building or [**redesigning your website**](/en/projects/website-redesign) is a smart move.
-   **Keyword research**: they identify the keywords that matter for your industry, to attract the right audience.
-   **On-page optimisation**: they improve the internal elements of the site (titles, meta tags, content structure, keywords, internal linking) to better meet search engines' criteria.
-   **Off-page optimisation**: they build backlink and link-building strategies to strengthen your site's authority in the eyes of search engines.
-   **Tracking and reporting**: they use analytics tools to monitor the site's performance (keyword rankings, organic traffic, conversion rate) and adjust the strategy accordingly.

## **Essential tools and resources for SEO consultants**

A freelance SEO consultant typically relies on a whole toolbox to do the job well. Here are some of the tools and resources they use:

-   **Google Analytics**: this free tool provides valuable insights into your website's performance, including traffic, conversions and traffic sources.
-   **Semrush**: this paid tool covers keyword research, competitive analysis and keyword rank tracking.
-   **Ubersuggest**: this software offers a range of search optimisation tools, including keyword research, website audits and rank tracking.
-   **Ahrefs**: another popular tool for keyword research, competitive analysis and backlink monitoring.

Beyond the tools, staying current with the latest SEO trends and best practices matters just as much. As a client, you can do the same on your side by following SEO blogs and podcasts, or subscribing to newsletters on the topic.
