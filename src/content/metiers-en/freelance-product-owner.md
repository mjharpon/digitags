The freelance Product Owner (PO) is responsible for managing a product's entire lifecycle, from design to delivery. They work closely with the development teams, stakeholders and customers to guarantee the product's success. Their expertise helps companies optimise their products and reach their business goals.

What skills does the role require, and what are the steps to follow? How do you pick a good product owner? We explain it all!

## **What is a Product Owner?**

The Product Owner is a key member of the development team, responsible for maximising the value of the product the team builds. Working closely with stakeholders (customers, users, leadership and more), the PO makes sure the product being developed answers the market's needs and the company's strategic goals.

The Product Owner role is often confused with the [**Product Manager**](/en/roles/freelance-product-manager)'s, but it focuses more on the operational management of product development, whereas the Product Manager is more involved in long-term strategy.

## **The skills of a product owner**

The freelance product owners in our hub of 1,600+ experts share a set of essential skills. Here are the key skills of a good PO:

### **Product backlog management**

As a freelance product owner, the expert needs solid product management experience. The backlog is a prioritised list of the work items for the development team. The Product Owner is responsible for defining the product vision, planning the product's development, prioritising features, collaborating with stakeholders and making strategic decisions to ensure the product succeeds. They make sure every item is clear, detailed and ready to be developed.

### **Communication skills**

Communication is essential to being a good product owner. They must communicate effectively with the development team, stakeholders and customers, and present their ideas clearly and convincingly to win the support their projects need.

### **Team spirit**

As a product owner — especially a freelance one — they need an entrepreneurial mindset: taking initiative, solving problems creatively and finding opportunities in challenges. They must also manage risks and make decisions that maximise the projects' chances of success.

### **Technical skills**

While they don't need to be a [**web developer**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer) or a technical expert, a general understanding of the technical concepts behind product management matters. It lets them communicate effectively with the development team and grasp the technical stakes of the product.
