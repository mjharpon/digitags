You're investing in search advertising, but the cost per acquisition keeps climbing and the return on investment stays blurry? A **freelance Google Ads expert** turns your ad budget into a measurable growth lever — without an agency's fixed fees.

## What is a paid search expert?

Paid search (SEA, Search Engine Advertising) is advertising on search engines: Google Ads first and foremost, but also Microsoft Ads. The paid search expert designs, structures and optimises paid campaigns to capture demand that already exists — people actively searching for your products or services.

Unlike [organic search](/en/roles/freelance-seo-consultant), which builds lasting visibility, paid search delivers immediate results you can steer day by day: every euro invested is traceable all the way to the conversion.

## The missions of a freelance Google Ads expert

-   Audit of your ad account: structure, wasted budget, missed opportunities.
-   Acquisition strategy: choice of networks (Search, Shopping, YouTube, Display, Performance Max), audiences and bidding.
-   Campaign structuring: ad groups, keywords, responsive ads, extensions.
-   Continuous optimisation: smart bidding, Quality Score, exclusions, A/B testing of the ads.
-   Landing pages: recommendations to align the landing page with the search intent.
-   Conversion tracking: a reliable measurement plan (GA4, Google Tag Manager, enhanced conversions).
-   Transparent reporting: CPA, ROAS, impression share — business indicators, not vanity metrics.

## When to bring in a freelance Google Ads expert?

-   You're launching a product or service and need qualified traffic fast.
-   Your campaigns are running but the CPA keeps rising and you don't know why.
-   You're bringing acquisition in-house and need a senior profile to structure and train the team.
-   You want ROI-driven management, with a controlled budget and clear reporting.

## The skills to look for

A good paid search expert combines technical mastery of the platforms (Google Ads certifications, scripts, Shopping feeds), a solid analytics culture to measure what matters, and business sense to shift budgets to where they pay off. The best profiles work hand in hand with the [traffic manager](/en/roles/freelance-traffic-manager) and the [social ads expert](/en/roles/freelance-social-ads-expert) for a coherent acquisition strategy across every channel.
