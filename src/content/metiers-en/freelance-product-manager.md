### **What is a product manager?**

A product manager is responsible for defining, developing and evolving a product or service. Their role is to make sure the product answers customers' needs, stays aligned with the company's goals and stands out from the competition. They work closely with many teams: development, design, marketing, sales and customer support.

The product manager is often described as the "CEO of the product". They must understand the market and the customers, define a clear product vision, set a strategy and coordinate the teams to deliver that vision.

## **The responsibilities of a freelance product manager**

Here are some of a product manager's main responsibilities:

### **Building the product strategy**

The PM is responsible for developing the product strategy. That means identifying market needs, running competitive research, defining the product's goals and creating a plan to reach them. Ultimately, it's about shaping a clear product vision by understanding the market, customers' needs and industry trends — then setting a product roadmap that prioritises features and improvements by their potential impact.

### **Project management**

The product manager also wears a [**project manager**](/en/roles/freelance-project-manager) hat, as they are responsible for managing the project end to end. That involves planning the project's stages, coordinating the team, tracking deadlines and solving the problems that come up along the way.

### **Data analysis**

The freelance product manager analyses data to make decisions. That can include market data, user data and product performance data. They use these insights to optimise the product's features and improve its success on the market.

### **Stakeholder collaboration**

The product manager works as part of a team and needs strong communication skills. They collaborate with stakeholders to understand needs, communicate the decisions made and resolve the issues that arise.

### **Product performance optimisation**

Beyond overseeing the product's development from concept to launch and beyond, the PM is responsible for optimising its performance. That can mean monitoring key metrics, proposing improvements and rolling out strategies to increase user satisfaction and the product's profitability.
