Looking to build an effective product strategy for your company?

A well-crafted product strategy is essential: it lets you clearly define your products' characteristics, target the right customers and stand out from your competitors. But how do you build one?

## How to build a product strategy?

![How to build a product strategy?](/uploads/projets/strategie-produit-0.webp)

## **Understanding why a good product strategy matters**

Planning to launch a new product, or improve an existing one? Here are a few questions to ask before you start.

The first step is understanding your market and your customers. What are their needs and expectations? What are the market trends? With that information in hand, you can build products that answer those needs and stand out from the competition.

Next, define your business goals. What do you want to achieve with your products? Grow sales, build customer loyalty, win new markets? Set realistic, measurable goals to guide your strategy.

Finally, create a detailed action plan to roll out your product strategy. Define the concrete steps to follow, the resources needed and the deadlines to meet.

## **The key stages of building a product strategy**

Building an effective product strategy takes a methodical, thoughtful approach. Let's go through the stages mentioned above in detail.

### **Market and competitive analysis**

Run a thorough analysis of the market and the competition. Who are the main players? Which similar products already exist? What are their strengths and weaknesses? This analysis helps you understand the market's trends and determine how to stand out from the competition.

### **Defining the goals of your product strategy**

Once you have a clear understanding of the market and the competition, you can define your product strategy's goals. They should be specific, measurable, achievable, relevant and time-bound (SMART goals).

### **Identifying your target audience and its needs**

Identify your target audience and understand its needs. Who are your ideal customers? What are their product needs and preferences? This stage lets you tailor your product strategy to your target audience's specific needs.

### **Developing a unique value proposition**

A unique value proposition is what sets your product apart from the competitors'. What makes your product unique for your target audience? What are its key benefits? Develop a clear, convincing value proposition that highlights your product's unique advantages.

### **Developing a pricing strategy**

Your product's price is a key element. Set a price that matches the value your product brings to your target audience while staying competitive. Naturally, factor in production costs, target margins and your competitors' pricing strategies.

### **Product design and development**

Once your value proposition is defined, you can move to designing and developing the product. That's where the development team comes in, building a product that answers your target audience's needs and reflects the unique advantages you've defined.

### **Rolling out a marketing and communication strategy**

Once your product is ready to launch, put an effective marketing and communication strategy in place. Make sure to tailor your message to your target audience's needs and preferences.

### **Tracking and evaluating your product strategy's effectiveness**

Once your product is on the market, tracking and evaluating its effectiveness matters. Use predefined KPIs to measure your product's success, and adjust your product strategy based on those measurements to maximise the results.
