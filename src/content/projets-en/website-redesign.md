Redesigning a website is a major undertaking, from the initial diagnosis to planning, from design to the final launch. Here is some practical advice to maximise the success of your website redesign.

Whether you want to modernise your site's look, improve the user experience or optimise performance for search engines, this guide is for you.

## **Why does your website redesign matter?**

Whether you're a small business, a start-up or a large company, optimising is essential. An outdated website can hurt your brand image and your ability to attract new clients.

Redesigning your website also helps you adapt to new market trends and answer your users' changing needs. By offering a good online experience, you can increase user engagement, generate leads and grow conversions.

## The stages of a website redesign project

Here are the stages we go through when running a website redesign project with a client.

### **Audit of your current website**

Before diving into your website redesign, it's important to assess your site's current state. We take the time to analyse your website's performance, including traffic, bounce rates and conversions. We identify the weak points and the potential problems a redesign can solve.

By examining your website's statistics, we can determine which pages are the most popular, which features are the most used and which gaps need filling. That gives us a better understanding of what works well on your site and what needs improving.

### **Defining the goals of your website redesign**

Before starting your website redesign, it's essential to define a clear goal. What do you want to achieve with this redesign? Do you want to modernise your site's look? Improve the user experience? Optimise your site for search engines?

With a clear goal defined, we can better plan your redesign and make the right decisions about your website's design, structure and content.

### **Keyword research to optimise your new website**

We identify the keywords your target audience actually searches for and weave them strategically into your new website's content. That helps you rank better in search results and attract qualified traffic.

### UX design: rethinking the site's structure

UX design plays a central role in your website redesign. Before starting, it's crucial to understand users' expectations, needs and behaviours — through methods such as surveys, interviews, user testing and analysis of existing data. The [**UX designer**](/en/roles/freelance-ux-designer) gathers this information to identify friction points and opportunities for improvement.

UX design means rethinking the site's structure and navigation so they are intuitive. That can include simplifying the menus, reorganising the content and laying out clearer user journeys. Good information architecture helps users easily find what they're looking for, reducing bounce rates and increasing time spent on the site.

The UX designer works closely with the [**web developers**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer) and the [**UI designer**](/en/roles/freelance-ui-designer) to make sure the designs are technically feasible and visually appealing. Weaving UX principles into the code and the UI design helps create a site that is not only functional but beautiful — and accessible.

### **UI design: creating an attractive design**

Your website's design is one of the first things users notice when they visit. A modern, attractive design helps create a strong first impression and strengthens your brand's credibility.

During your site's redesign, we craft a design that matches your brand identity and suits your target audience. We use appealing visual elements, a clear layout and easy-to-read typography to offer your users a pleasant visual experience.
