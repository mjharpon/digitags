Wondering how to improve your website's user experience? A UX audit can help you identify your site's shortcomings and roll out meaningful improvements to make it more engaging.

User experience plays a major role, directly influencing user satisfaction, loyalty and conversion. A UX audit lets you assess ease of navigation, clarity of information, accessibility, loading speed and many other essential aspects of your site.

## UX audit

![UX audit](/uploads/projets/audit-ux-0.webp)

## **What is a UX audit?**

A UX audit is a complete evaluation of your website to identify the problems and gaps that may hurt your users' experience. It's an in-depth analysis that examines every aspect of your site. The main goal of a UX audit is to determine how to improve your site to deliver an exceptional user experience. A UX audit is recommended when planning a [**website redesign**](/en/projects/website-redesign).

A UX audit typically includes a thorough study of how users interact with your site, analysing the data and running tests. That brings to light the specific problems that may cause users frustration or confusion.

## **Why user experience matters**

It's a constant battle: how to offer the most pleasant user experience possible. When a user visits your website, they expect a smooth experience. A poor user experience can drive your bounce rate up. An exceptional one, on the other hand, strengthens user satisfaction, builds loyalty and increases your conversions.

Investing in user experience is therefore essential to your website's long-term success. To do so, you can bring in an experienced [**freelance UX designer**](/en/roles/freelance-ux-designer), who can support you through the audit and the improvements that follow.

## **Why run a UX audit?**

Here are some of the main benefits of running a thorough UX audit:

1.  Identify problems : a UX audit pinpoints the specific issues that may hurt the user experience, such as navigation, readability, slowness or accessibility problems.
2.  Improve the experience : with targeted improvements, you make your site friendlier and interaction with your content easier.
3.  Increase conversions : an improved user experience can directly impact your site's conversion rates. By making your site friendlier, highlighting the CTAs and optimising the forms, you encourage users to act — such as completing a purchase.
4.  Strengthen user retention : when users have a positive experience on your site, they're more likely to come back and become loyal users.
5.  Build a competitive edge : an exceptional user experience can set you apart from the competition and attract more users to your site.

## **The stages of a UX audit**

Before starting a UX audit, it's essential to clearly define your goals. Ask yourself: What do you want to achieve with this audit? Which problems do you want to solve? Which objectives do you want to reach?

Once your goals are set, you can move on to the next stages.

### **Data collection for the UX audit**

Data collection is the first stage of a UX audit. It gives you valuable information about how users interact with your website.

Many collection methods are available: user interviews, user testing, analysis of existing data, online surveys and more. Each has its pros and cons — pick the one that best fits your needs and resources.

During collection, make sure to gather information on the following: how easy the site is to use, user satisfaction, problems encountered, reasons for visiting, users' expectations and so on. The more data you collect, the more material you'll have to analyse and decide on.

### **Analysis of the collected data**

Analysing the collected data is one of the most important stages of a UX audit. This is where you examine the data in detail to identify the problems and the opportunities for improvement.

During the analysis, identify the trends and patterns that emerge from the data to get a global view of the situation.

Use analysis aids such as charts, tables and diagrams to visualise and understand the data more easily. Note the recurring problems and your website's strengths, and use that information to shape improvement recommendations.

### **Recommendations and UX action plan**

Once the problems and opportunities are identified, you can move to the next stage: the recommendations and the action plan.

The expert can then formulate specific recommendations to fix the identified problems and seize the improvement opportunities. Make sure the recommendations are achievable and aligned with your goals.

Set a detailed action plan to implement them. Identify the resources needed, the responsibilities and the deadlines for each task.

### **Implementing the UX changes**

With the help of the [**UX designer**](/en/roles/freelance-ux-designer), start with the priority recommendations and follow your action plan. Involve the necessary stakeholders — designers, [**developers**](/en/roles/freelance-web-developer), marketers and so on. Make sure the goals and expectations are clearly communicated to everyone involved in the implementation.

Make the necessary changes to your website, following design and development best practices. Test the new features and improvements to make sure they work properly and genuinely improve the user experience.

### **Monitoring and evaluating the UX improvements**

For this final stage, use analytics tools to track your website's performance after the changes. Analyse the data to see whether the improvements had a positive impact on the user experience. If needed, make further adjustments to maximise the results.

Keep monitoring and evaluating your website's performance regularly to maintain an optimal user experience.
